# Art-director review — 23-wildcard3

**Score:** 8/10

**Verdict:** Blazon is an ownable and strictly built system. Every title reads at 180px, level and field are encoded cleanly, and most shields are true to their mechanism, but a few execution slips (a pluralisation bug, a clipped charge, Or charges on the Or ground) and an Ordinary that already contradicts itself keep it short of finished.

**Strongest:** build-llms-from-scratch. "Per bend grady of eight Argent and Gules" is the causal mask drawn literally: row 0 sees only itself and row 7 is fully red, exactly as an 8×8 lower-triangular mask should be. It is one bold two-tincture division that holds at 180px, in greyscale and at 32px on the wrap's spine, and its one-line blazon proves the whole "code beside its output" idea better than any rationale. pi-vs-hermes-vs-codex is a close second: three marshalled escutcheons that make a comparison book legible at a glance.

**Weakest:** kernel-engineering. The "in trick" shield reads as an empty white spreadsheet on black at 180px. The bar, pallet and output tile, which are the whole idea, differ from the field lattice only by a 5px versus 2px stroke. The sa/or/gu labels are about 6px tall in the thumbnail. It is also the only advanced cover without the Or rim, so it drops out of the advanced family.

## Must fix

1. **pi-vs-hermes-vs-codex** — The extent line reads "9 capsules · 1 hours", a grammatical error printed on a core-six cover.
   - *Fix:* Pluralise in coverInner: `${b.hours} hour${b.hours === 1 ? '' : 's'}` (do the same for capsules), then re-render.
2. **charlie-vision-room** — The bar compony in base (shield space x 80–320, y 358–418; on the cover about x 356–596, y 698–758) runs into the curved flanks of the shield. Both lower corners are cut off by the outline, which is a collision you can see at full size.
   - *Fix:* Move the device up so the bar clears the flanks by at least 12px, e.g. quarterly square y0 = 150 and bar by = 330. Or shrink the token cells to bw = 52 and keep the bar centred.
3. **decision-trees-from-scratch** — Virginica is charged with mullets, but the proposal's own Ordinary defines the mullet as 'the query' (it is the query in rag-in-production), and the same mullet is the third-son cadency mark in charlie-sound-room. The system rests on 'one meaning per charge, so the language is learnable', and the second foundations shield a reader meets already breaks it.
   - *Fix:* Make every iris a data point, coloured by class: hurts on the Or pale (setosa), torteaux on the Argent chief (virginica), plates on the Azure base (versicolor). This keeps the rule of tincture and the Ordinary (roundel = data point), then re-blazon: '...in dexter three hurts in pale, in sinister chief two torteaux, in sinister base three plates'. Alternatively keep the lozenges for 'another class' and swap only the mullets for torteaux.

## Improvements (highest impact first)

1. **system** — Fix the shield-on-ground edge for both coloured grounds. (1) Intermediate: on the Or ground, an Or charge touching the shield edge is metal on metal against the ground, which breaks the rule of tincture the proposal leans on. The CNN canton reads as a bitten-off corner, and the Vision and Sound factory chiefs dissolve into the ground at 180px and fully in greyscale. Give every shield on the Or ground a 10–12px Argent fimbriation, mirroring the Or rim on sable, or at least a 10px sable outline. (2) Advanced: the 12px Or rim is stroked on the shield path, so advanced shields measure 270–681 × 334 against 276–675 × 340 on the others. They sit 38px from the right trim and poke past the title and Nº measure at 676. Draw the rim inside the 400×480 box (shield at 388 wide, offset 6px, or stroke an inset path) so every shield on every level has one outer box.
2. **system** — Break up the Azure-and-Or monotony and catch the look-alikes now, not after 50 are drawn. 6 of the 17 shields have Azure fields, 5 of them Azure with Or charges on paper, and the drafted roll adds about 8 more. The drafts already produce look-alikes at 180px. ML Fundamentals ('Azure, a bend Or between six plates') is nearly neural-networks-from-scratch ('Azure, on a bend Or ... between two plates'). DSA ('Azure, seven roundels Or') sits beside git ('Azure, a pall Or charged with seven hurts'), and DL Fundamentals' chevronels sit beside Math's chevron ployé. Suggested rule: code-and-data books (Git, SQL, Python, R, DSA, Modern Software Developer) carry Argent charges, or take a new tincture such as a murrey stain, while maths and ML keep Or. Redraft ML Fundamentals away from the bend (e.g. roundels scattered either side of a bend wavy, residuals). Run the 180px confusability review on the drafts before drawing them.
3. **charlie-reasoning-room** — Rethink the emblem. A staircase to a star is a motivational-poster metaphor, not the mechanism of RL. It also has three smaller problems: only 3 of the 4 blazoned steps are visible (the lowest is outside the shield curve), the estoile's right ray sits about 2px from the rim, and the martlet, the one figurative charge, reads as a crude bird or a submarine. Alternative in the book's own lexicon, covering 'from bandits to reasoning models': in fess five roundels Argent (five sampled attempts or arms pulled), the middle one Or and ensigned with an estoile (the only one rewarded). That is try many, reward what is right, the core of both bandits and GRPO-style reasoning training, and it reads as one bold row at 180px. Redraw the martlet from a proper heraldic model (footless, tufted), at least 130 units wide with the standard 3px outline.
4. **kernel-engineering** — Keep the tiled-matmul idea and the lovely 'in trick' conceit, but make the charges carry the drawing. Drop the lattice on the field. Draw the bar and the pallet as 8px outlines, each subdivided into 4 tiles, and outline the crossing tile at 10px with 'gu'. Set the tincture abbreviations at 34px or more so they survive print and thumbnails, and add the advanced Or rim (dashed if you want 'drafted') so coming-soon books stay in their level's family. Re-blazon to drop 'the field latticed in tiles'.
5. **charlie-sound-room** — The rounded-bar waveform is the generic voice-assistant UI icon, the least heraldic and least specific shield in the set. The Azure field also contradicts the tincture table (Azure = foundations; agents = Purpure). Make it about what a voice-agent book actually teaches, turn-taking and latency: two rows of pallets couped, the caller's in Argent in the upper half and the agent's in Or below, offset in time so the agent starts only after a clear empty gap (endpointing and response latency). Then either document a Charlie sub-series tincture rule in the Ordinary, or re-tincture so the table stays true.
