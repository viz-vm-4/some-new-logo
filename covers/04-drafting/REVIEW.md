# Art-director review — 04-drafting

**Score:** 7.5/10

**Verdict:** Drawing Office is a rigorous, ownable system: the title block, zone marks, one ink colour and the drafting conventions hold the shelf together, and most emblems draw the real mechanism. For a technical publisher it isn't ready to ship yet: the GPT-2 block is drawn in the wrong order, the Vision cover has the wrong patch count, symbols fall back to system fonts on 7 of 12 PDFs, two titles overflow their cell, and the Sound and Reasoning plans are missing tags their equipment schedules list.

**Strongest:** ai-context-engineering: the "Section A–A through one context window" idea maps the subject exactly. The fixed ink diameter is the window limit, the strands are instructions, tools, knowledge and memory, the jacketed strand is the isolated sub-agent, and the four strategies (write, select, compress, isolate) are called out in ink. The drafting is honest: a side view with cutting plane A–A, then the section. The heavy vermilion ring is the most legible emblem on the shelf at 180px and still reads in greyscale. (Kernel Engineering is a close second: its A/B strips, output tile and k-step slices are consistent with each other.)

**Weakest:** charlie-sound-room: the emblem is a row of labelled boxes (VAD, ASR, LLM, TTS) on a conveyor. It reads as a generic flowchart, not a drawing, and uses the least drafting craft of any cover. The equipment schedule lists tags 1–4 but only tag 1 is on the plan. The UNITS cell says "ms" yet no millisecond value appears anywhere. At 180px it can't be told apart from its three siblings: a black room outline with small grey contents.

## Must fix

1. **build-llms-from-scratch** — The GPT-2 block is drawn upside-down relative to its own data flow. Flow goes up (token IDs → embedding at the bottom, LM head → NEXT TOKEN at the top). Read bottom to top, each block is FFN·GELU → LayerNorm → MASKED MHA → LayerNorm, so the feed-forward runs before attention. The ⊕ residual joins also sit on the LN bars (post-norm), and GPT-2 is pre-LN. The top LN of Block 12 then stacks directly under ln_f, giving two norms in a row. The caption says 'GPT-2 (124M), general arrangement', so any reader who knows the architecture will spot it.
   - *Fix:* In block(), reorder the rows top to bottom as FFN·GELU, LN2, MASKED MHA, LN1. Residual 1 branches below LN1 and rejoins with ⊕ just above the MHA. Residual 2 branches there and rejoins with ⊕ above the FFN. Move cutPlane B–B (currently y=210) to the MHA's new position, and move the 'MASKED ATTENTION / 12 HEADS' and 'FEED-FORWARD' leaders to match.
2. **charlie-vision-room** — The image is cut into 4×4 = 16 patches, but only 12 patch tokens follow [CLS] on the conveyor (the loop is `for (let i = 0; i < 12; i++)`). Flattening 16 patches cannot give 12 tokens, and 'patches are tokens' is the point of this cover.
   - *Fix:* Draw 16 tokens (at a 16px pitch they run from 146 to ~402 and clear the encoder at 418) and keep index 6 red, which matches the lifted patch at row 1, col 2. Alternatively, draw a few tokens, a conventional break, and a note reading '16 + [CLS]'.
3. **system (ai-context-engineering, build-llms-from-scratch, mathematical-foundations-for-ml, neural-networks-from-scratch, charlie-reasoning-room, charlie-vision-room, decision-trees-from-scratch)** — Glyph fallback. These 7 PDFs embed DejaVuSansMono or DejaVuSansMono-Bold, and the NN PDF also embeds LiberationSerif. The visible cases: the Σ in NN Detail A is set in Instrument Sans, which has no Greek, so it renders as a serif Σ, the only serif glyph on the shelf. The pipe in the Reasoning room's 'π(a|s)' is a tall DejaVu bar that pokes above cap height. The subscripts (x₁, t₂, λ₁, aₜ, sₜ₊₁), ≤, → and ⌀ also come from DejaVu. These glyphs depend on the rendering machine, so the web library will show different glyphs on Mac and Windows.
   - *Fix:* Add a subscript helper to Tx that uses <tspan baseline-shift='sub' font-size='72%'> with Overpass Mono digits and letters. Draw Σ, ⌀, ≤ and → as SVG paths in the kit, as you already did for −∞. Or load one covering Google font (e.g. Noto Sans Math) and name it explicitly as the second family in F. Then re-check with `strings *.pdf | grep FontName` until only Instrument Sans and Overpass Mono appear.
4. **ai-context-engineering, kernel-engineering** — ts:72 overflows the title cell. The cell has ~180px of content height, and label + 2×72×0.97 + desc needs ~191px. The subtitle is pushed to ~3px above the title-block rule: the descenders of 'system' and 'speculative' nearly touch it. It also sits ~8px under the 'g' of 'Engineering'. On the other 10 covers the subtitle sits ~18px clear of the rule, so its baseline jumps between covers.
   - *Fix:* Cap two-line titles at 66px (NN at 66 fits). Also pin .desc to a fixed baseline (e.g. position:absolute; bottom:18px in .tb-title) so the subtitle sits at the same y on every cover whatever the title does.
5. **charlie-sound-room, charlie-reasoning-room** — Each equipment schedule lists tags 1–4, but only balloon 1 is placed on the plan. Sound is missing ASR, LLM·TTS and LATENCY. Reasoning is missing AGENT, ENVIRONMENT and REWARD. The red tag 4, the one ink item in the schedule, has nothing to point to. Language and Vision tag all four, so this breaks the sub-series' own convention.
   - *Fix:* Place bal(…,2), bal(…,3) and a red bal(…,4) on both plans. Sound: tag 2 to ASR, tag 3 to the LLM/TTS pair, red tag 4 on the LATENCY BUDGET dimension line. Reasoning: tag 2 to AGENT, tag 3 to ENV, red tag 4 on the reward arrow.
6. **decision-trees-from-scratch** — The threshold dimensions are wrong. t₂ runs from the top edge of the feature space down to the split, but x₂ increases upward, so a threshold on x₂ must be measured from the bottom edge (the origin). t₃ is chained from the t₁ line, so as drawn it reads as t₃ − t₁. The Gini values also don't match the 18 plotted points (7○, 7●, 4△). The root should be 0.65 (shown 0.66), the x₂≤t₂ node 0.46 (shown 0.49), and the x₁≤t₃ node 0.49 (shown 0.44). This book's UNITS cell is literally 'Gini'.
   - *Fix:* Use baseline dimensioning from one datum corner: t₁ and t₃ both from the left edge (stack t₃ on a second dimension row below t₁), and t₂ from the bottom edge on the right side. Compute the gini labels from the point arrays in draw() rather than typing them in.
7. **neural-networks-from-scratch** — Detail A enlarges a first-hidden-layer neuron, which receives 4 inputs, but draws only 3 weighted inputs (w₁, w₂, w₃).
   - *Fix:* Draw 4 input arrows labelled w₁–w₄ (the detail circle has room if you fan them over ±40°), or draw 3 and label the last one wₙ with a short ellipsis.
8. **mathematical-foundations-for-ml** — The 'λ₂ = 0.63' dimension label sits inside the ellipse on top of the red eigen-axis. Its paper knock-out cuts a visible gap in the 2.6px ellipse outline where '0.63' crosses it. 'λ₁ = 1.97' likewise knocks a gap in the unit circle.
   - *Fix:* Take the λ₂ dimension outside the ellipse. Extend extension lines from the origin and the minor-axis end perpendicular to v₂, set the dimension line beyond the ellipse's lower-right edge, and put the label there. Nudge λ₁'s label along its line so it clears the unit circle.

## Improvements (highest impact first)

1. **system** — Replace hand-tuned title sizes with a rule. There are currently 9 different ts values between 44 and 72 across 12 covers, so cap height and title-block rhythm jump from cover to cover, and ts:72 overflows. Define 3 steps, e.g. 66px for ≤2 short lines, 54px for 2 long lines, 44px for 3 lines. Choose the step automatically by measuring the longest line against the 600px measure. Fix the title top and subtitle baselines too. That lets a new book's cover be 'data + one drawing function', as NOTES promises.
2. **build-llms-from-scratch, 5d-parallelism** — Make line breaks follow one rule: break before prepositions and never split a compound noun. Build LLMs currently splits 'Large Language / Models'; set it as 'Build Large Language Models / (LLMs) from Scratch' (both lines are ~26 characters and fit at 52px). 5D ends line 1 on 'for' while Math correctly breaks before it; set it as '5D Parallelism / for Large Model Training'.
3. **charlie-* sub-series** — Equipment tags and the column-grid bubbles are both ~9.5px circles, so '1' and '2' appear twice with different meanings on every plan. Follow architectural practice and make equipment tags a distinct shape, e.g. a hexagon or a circle split by a horizontal line. At 180px the four rooms are nearly identical black rectangles told apart only by the tiny key-plan cell. Scale the interior equipment up to fill the room, and make each room's ink element a solid mass of at least ~40px so the four read as four books.
4. **charlie-sound-room, charlie-reasoning-room** — These are the weakest emblems; rethink them. Sound: turn the box row into a chain dimension. Each station's width becomes proportional to its latency share and is dimensioned in ms (e.g. VAD 200 · ASR 300 · LLM 350 · TTS 150), with the overall budget as the single ink dimension. That uses real drafting and makes the 'ms' units cell true. Reasoning: draw the room's floor as a gridworld, with each tile carrying its V(s) value, internal walls, a goal tile, and the greedy policy path as ink arrows ending at +reward. It is a genuine floor plan and a genuine RL object, whereas the agent/env racetrack is a generic textbook loop.
5. **system** — Enforce 'one quantity in ink', and make that quantity a mass that survives 180px and greyscale. Math currently inks the eigen-axes, both λ dims, the det A area and its label. The Language room inks both the decode loop and the KV cache. In greyscale, the Math legend's 'BEFORE A' phantom and 'EIGENVECTORS' chain become indistinguishable, and on Build LLMs the red-hatched MHA merges with the grey LayerNorm hatch. Keep one ink element per cover, separate the other lines by weight or dash pattern rather than by hue, and give the ink element weight: solid-fill the allowed cells in Section B–B, fill Pi's summaries solid and thicken the LIMIT datum to 3px. Those two covers, Math and the rooms currently recede into grey line texture on the contact sheet.
6. **system** — Clean up the drafting-convention slips, since the whole premise is 'real conventions used honestly':
- Kernel: 'SECTION M–M' has no cutting plane M anywhere on the sheet. Rename it 'DETAIL M' or add the plane.
- Build LLMs: the −∞ is drawn at 2px, heavier than all the lettering, and grid lines run through it. Size it to the 10px lettering and knock it out.
- Reasoning: the ENV box is pattern-only, so the grey track shows through it. Put a paper fill under the hatch.
- Kernel: the PRELIMINARY stamp comes within ~5px of the right border. Pull it in 12–16px.
- Frame: the bottom zone numerals sit 22px from trim while the top ones sit 26px. Make them symmetric at ≥26px.
- 5D: route the 'RANK 0' leader so it doesn't cross three cube outlines.
