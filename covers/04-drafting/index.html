Vizuara Books · cover system · proposal 04

Drawing Office

Every Vizuara book is issued as an engineering drawing of the machine it teaches you to build.

Vizuara’s promise is that you build the thing yourself, so each cover is the sheet you would build it from: the book’s core mechanism drawn with a drafting office’s conventions — a neural net dimensioned as a hole pattern, a context window cut in section, the causal mask as Section B–B, three compaction strategies as three ways to shorten a shaft.

The system lives in the title block. Drawing number = book number, level is stamped as filled squares, capsules and hours are the drawing’s extent, the units note says what the drawing is measured in, and Vizuara signs in the corner where a drawing office signs its work. One paper, one graphite, one engineering ink. The drawing changes every time; the sheet never does.

IMPRINT

The mark is a V-block — the machinist’s fixture that cradles round work so it can be measured — drawn in section, holding one part up for inspection. It reads as a V at any size and is always set at the foot of the title block, never above the title.

Type

Instrument Sans
SemiBold, width 88 — the title. The book, set like a product name on a Braun manual.
OVERPASS MONO
Lettering, dimensions, title block. Descended from Highway Gothic — the same lineage as the drafting template.

Level

Stamped in ink in the LEVEL cell, like a tolerance class: the more squares filled, the tighter the work. Always spelled out beside it.

Line conventions

Real drafting rules, used honestly: phantom lines for “before”, chain lines for axes, hatching as materials, balloons keyed to parts. No line thinner than 0.9 px at trim.

Drawing register

Print wrap — VB·003, back · spine · front

The back is the parts list: chapters listed as items with their capsule count, numbered from the bottom up the way a bill of materials grows from the title block.
