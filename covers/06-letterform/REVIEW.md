# Art-director review — 06-letterform

**Score:** 8/10

**Verdict:** This is a distinctive, disciplined system: one character per book, resting on one rule, with level shown by how much ink is on the cover. At 180px the shelf reads as one family, and every core-six emblem is true to its subject. It needs a revision round for a fallback glyph on transformers-from-scratch, renders that are 1px too tall, a forthcoming treatment that vanishes on black, and looser baseline and trim discipline than its own rules claim. It does not need a rethink.

**Strongest:** pi-vs-hermes-vs-codex. It is the hardest title in the core set: three rival agents, a set-up that invites logos. The designer solves it with one true sign, ≈ (U+2248), because compaction replaces a history with something only approximately equal to it. The ≈ is cropped off both edges, reads instantly at 180px, and the three-line title breaks cleanly after the colon. mathematical-foundations-for-ml (∂) is the cleanest execution of the system's own rule: whole character, sitting on the line, exact caption.

**Weakest:** kernel-engineering. It is the template for all three forthcoming books, and all three are advanced, so they all get the worst-contrast ground. Hatching #3548EE at 3px lines on an 8px pitch over #121212 averages about 1.7:1 contrast. The emblem turns into a dim haze at 180px and nearly disappears in greyscale (checked). The `__glo / bal__` break splits the keyword mid-word, and both underscore runs end 10px from the trim.

## Must fix

1. **transformers-from-scratch** — The transpose sign ⊤ is a font fallback. The PDF embeds DejaVuSans because the Google Fonts subsets of Libertinus Serif do not include U+22A4. The result is a thin monoline sans tack beside high-contrast Libertinus italic 𝑄𝐾, visible at full size. On other machines it will be yet another system font. NOTES wrongly says it comes from Libertinus Serif. The QK⊤ cell in the back-cover chart has the same fallback.
   - *Fix:* Set the transpose as a superscript roman capital T from Libertinus Serif (U+0054, which is in the latin subset), giving 𝑄𝐾ᵀ as most papers write it. Alternatively, convert Libertinus Math's ⊤ to an SVG path. Update the caption codepoints to match, re-render, and confirm that `strings pdf/transformers-from-scratch.pdf | grep FontName` no longer lists DejaVu.
2. **all (system)** — Every JPG and PNG is 720×889 (1440×1778 at 2×), not 720×888. The covers sit at fractional y positions on the presentation page, so each render includes a 1px strip of page colour #E6E2D8. It is along the head on 10 covers, where it shows as a pale hairline on all five blue ones, and along the foot on 7. These JPGs are what the web library will show.
   - *Fix:* Give everything above each cover an integer height, for example a fixed `height:120px` on figcaption and integer px line-heights in .sec and .lede. Or nudge each .cover's top to a whole pixel in JS after layout. Re-render and check that every JPG is 720×888 with no #E6E2D8 rows.
3. **kernel-engineering** — The forthcoming hatch leaves the emblem at about 1.7:1 average contrast on black. At 180px `__glo bal__` is a dim haze, and in greyscale it nearly disappears (checked). Every coming-soon title in books.json is advanced, so all three forthcoming covers will hit this worst case. The first underscore run also starts 10px inside the left trim and `bal__` ends 10px inside the right trim, within normal guillotine tolerance, so the literal token can lose part of an underscore.
   - *Fix:* Keep the hatch but give each hatched glyph a 2px outline in --gl so its edges stay defined, or hatch in paper (the title ink) on dark grounds. Tighten the pattern to 3px lines on a 5px pitch. Then either bleed both runs well past the trim (e.g. ix:-24 and ix:744) or pull them in to the 36px margin.
4. **build-llms-from-scratch, charlie-reasoning-room** — The system promises that the caption 'names the character exactly', but two captions get the Unicode name wrong. U+0120 is 'LATIN CAPITAL LETTER G WITH DOT ABOVE' (the caption drops LETTER). U+1D53C is 'MATHEMATICAL DOUBLE-STRUCK CAPITAL E' (the caption drops MATHEMATICAL). Any type-literate reader will catch these.
   - *Fix:* Use the official Unicode names verbatim. Both still fit comfortably on the 648px caption line next to the level word. Add a build-time check of each caption against a table of Unicode names.

## Improvements (highest impact first)

1. **system** — Make rule 2 ('every character rests on the same rule') actually true. On the 2× renders, the gap between emblem ink and the rule is 9.5px on charlie-reasoning-room (𝔼 visibly floats), 8px on python, 5.5px on r-masterclass, 5px on diffusion-lm and transformers, about 3px on Ġ, σ, λ and ≤, and 0 on ∈, where it fuses into the rule. ≈ (pi-vs-hermes) and ∿ (charlie-sound-room) deliberately hover about 36px and 42px above it. Allow only two states: the character sits on the rule (0–1px, optical overshoot allowed) or the rule cuts it. Drop the iy offsets on python, pi and sound, or push ≈ and ∿ down until the rule cuts the lower trough, and move 𝔼 down 9px. Apply the same logic to the edges: an emblem either bleeds past the trim or stays 36px or more inside. Today it lands in between on charlie-vision-room ('16' about 12–18px from the head), transformers (⊤ 22px from the right), 5d-parallelism ('data' 18px from the left) and kernel (10px). Placing emblems by outlined-path bounds, which is already in 'what I'd do next', removes the canvas-metric drift.
2. **charlie-sound-room** — Rethink the emblem. ∿ is the generic 'sound = sine wave'. On the shelf it forms a trio of waves with ≈ (pi-vs-hermes) and ∼ (generative-ai-fundamentals) that will look alike. Use ℱ (U+2131 SCRIPT CAPITAL F), the Fourier transform: every voice agent starts by turning the waveform into a spectrogram. It is in Libertinus Math, its script silhouette is unlike anything else in the set, and it can be cropped by the head the way λ is.
3. **charlie-language-room** — The rationale for λ, 'the initial of λόγος', is an etymology pun, not the 'working heart' of the subtitle's subject, Inference Engineering. Keep the glyph and re-derive it truthfully: λ is the request arrival rate that every LLM serving system is batched and sized against. Say that in the back-cover blurb ('λ — request arrival rate'). Or switch to `</s>` in Chivo Mono, the end-of-sequence token every decode loop runs until. Either way, 𝐾𝑉 stays free for inference-engineering.
4. **python-for-data-science** — `>>>` says 'Python', not 'data science'. It is also the only emblem squeezed into a thin band: 366px of empty field sit above it, and it adds a third chevron shape to ≤ and ≻. Switch to `df`, the pandas DataFrame name used in almost every data-science notebook, set in Chivo Mono. Fit it to the same 470px height as ∂ and σ, and let the f crop at the right trim. If you keep `>>>`, fit it by height and let the third > crop off the right edge so it fills the field like its neighbours.
5. **system** — The 52-character chart shows collisions that will be visible once the shelf is full. ↻ (ai-agents-bootcamp) and ↺ (memory-in-ai-systems) are mirror images, and both sit on the blue ground. ∂ appears twice (math foundations and ∂𝑢 for sciml). 𝑄𝐾⊤, 𝐾𝑉 and 𝐾3 all lead with a huge italic K. Seven Chivo Mono bracket or chevron tokens ([1], [MASK], [CLS], <image>, {}, >_, >>>) share one silhouette. '&' for NLP & CV Mastery comes from the title's conjunction, not the subject. Write a rule that no two books in the same level share a silhouette family, and allow at most one bracketed token per level. Concrete swaps: memory-in-ai-systems → ↦ (key ↦ value, associative memory); sciml → 𝑢̇ (the time derivative a neural ODE learns).
6. **system** — Make the written spec match the covers. (a) Caption grammar: [1], >>> and 16×16 print codepoint lists, and 16×16 lists only 3 of its 5 characters, while [MASK] and __global__ print the literal token. Rule: single characters get U+XXXX plus the official name; multi-character tokens print literally plus their role ('[1] CONSOLE OUTPUT INDEX'). (b) 5d-parallelism is the only cover with no character and no codepoint. Either write it into the rules as the one 'word-run' exception, or anchor the caption with U+2225 PARALLEL TO. (c) The Charlie covers never say 'Charlie and the Intelligence Factory', so the Roman numeral has nothing to refer to. Put 'THE INTELLIGENCE FACTORY · II' in the caption's right slot beside the level. (d) NOTES says the design prints in two inks, but #3548EE on black is brighter than the #2233C8 spot and cannot be printed as a tint of it. Either specify a second spot for characters on black, or proof the advanced covers with the real spot. They will print darker than the render, and those emblems are already only 2.96:1 against black.
