Vizuara Books · Cover system proposal 16

Double Take

One idea per book. Each cover is a single visual thought — a pun, a metaphor, a figure–ground trick — built from a handful of flat shapes, that you get in a second and remember for a year. The idea has to be true: a joke that teaches the wrong thing about the subject doesn't make the cut.

Everything around the idea is deliberately quiet: one typeface, one title size, one hang line, three grounds. The shelf reads as a family because the system never changes; each book is findable because its picture never repeats.
