## Defects

In the 2x PNG the 'T' of 'THE INTELLIGENCE FACTORY · IV' is only 1px (0.5px at 1x) from the left notch, so at full size it touches the notch. Charlie III (Sound Room) has the same contact on its left notch. The ticket stays 256px wide while the text gets longer with the numeral: I has about 5.5px of clearance, II 2.5px, III and IV 0.5px.
  - *Fix:* In ticket(), size the badge to the text: either widen the ticket path (for example 256 to 272) or set letter-spacing 1.9 to about 1.3 for all four. Keep at least 6px between the text and each notch on I to IV.
- **charlie-language-room** (minor) — The emerging token is hidden by something the viewer can't see. The slot rect (452,420,20,80) is painted in ink after the token (424–484), so the token stops in a flat vertical cut at x=452. That cut faces a 20px strip of bare black ground, and the red machine starts only at x=472. Nothing visible covers the token, so it reads as a clipped yellow sliver. NOTES says it is 'drawn behind the machine', but that is not what the render shows. This is the same in the JPG and the PDF.
  - *Fix:* Delete the ink slot rect so the red body covers the token from x=452 (as the review proposed). Or give the slot a visible edge, for example keep a red lip at x 452–458 above and below the token. Either way the token's hidden part must be covered by red machine, not by ground colour.
- **pretraining-mini-kimi-k3** (minor) — The train is not fully on the rail, and there is a new hairline seam. (a) The engine's wheels sit at local x −54, −10 and 46, inside its ±66 chord points, so the curve's sag lifts them off the outer rail. I computed the gaps from the code at 1.3px, 4.0px and 1.9px, and they show as black slivers at full size. The two cars' wheels sit exactly on the rail (gap 0). (b) The cab rect (6,−142,72,110) ends at y=−32, the same line as the bottom of the black chassis. On the rotated engine this leaves a faint dashed red hairline (≈72,33,26 on 26,24,23) under the cab. It is the same kind of seam as review must-fix 8.
  - *Fix:* (a) Place each engine wheel at its own arc-length point on the rail and rotate it the same way the cars are placed. Or put the engine's wheels at its ±wb chord points, or drop them by the local sag. (b) End the cab at y=−36 so its bottom edge is inside the chassis, or extend the chassis 1px past the cab.
- **build-llms-from-scratch** (minor) — The caption says 'with a different script in every window', and NOTES says 'Every window holds a different script'. The image doesn't match.
