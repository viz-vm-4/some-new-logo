# Final proof — 03-pelican

All nine must-fix items check out in the renders. The JPGs are 720×888 and every PDF embeds only Schibsted Grotesk and matches its render. Level and capsule/hour counts match books.json. What remains is five minor issues: a hull polygon missing a corner on 5D, hairline seams on the Charlie roofs and at floor contacts, and three caption or rule contradictions (Transformers ink arcs, the Kernel 'keylines only' caption, red on the shade in the Sound room). None of them blocks print.

Review must-fixes confirmed fixed: 9

## Defects

- **5d-parallelism** (minor) — The dark shade backdrop behind the 5-cube (the 'zonotope' hull polygon) is missing a corner. The code picks hull support directions at a = k·36°, and each of those is exactly perpendicular to one axis. The sign is then decided by floating-point noise (dot ≈ ±1e-15), so hull[1] and hull[2] both land on field (479.6, 414.6) and the corner at field (558.3, ~306), page (558, 678), is skipped. The result is a light field-coloured wedge between page (558,544), (558,678) and (480,787) on the right side only, with no match on the left. It shows in both the JPG and the PDF.
  - *Fix:* In ART['5d-parallelism'], offset the support directions by half a step (const a = k*Math.PI/5 + Math.PI/10) so no direction ties with an axis. Or build the polygon as the convex hull of the 32 points in pts. Then re-render the JPG, PNG and PDF.
- **charlie-language-room / charlie-vision-room / charlie-sound-room / charlie-reasoning-room** (minor) — A hairline seam in the field colour runs the full width of the roof at page y 462, where the sawtooth polygons (bottom at base=90) meet the roof-beam rect (top at base=90). In the 2x PNGs it is a clear 1-device-px green or blue line: row 924 reads (42,59,48) against ink (28,27,25). In the JPGs it is faint (about 44 vs 28). The same abutment seam appears where ink shapes stand on the ink floor at page y 840 (maths matrix and histogram, RAG shelf, Language machine, Vision tokens, Transformers). The cause is that the covers still sit about 0.25 px off the pixel grid: row 0 of every JPG is about 25% page colour, and the band edges anti-alias.
  - *Fix:* Overlap the abutting shapes. In factory(), draw the roof polygons down to base+2 (or draw the beam rect after the roof), and extend grounded shapes 1–2 px into the floor. Optionally make snapped() in tools/render.js round to device pixels, so the cover lands exactly on the grid. Re-render.
- **transformers-from-scratch** (minor) — The caption on the page and the NOTES say the ink head 'reaches back to the start', but one of its four ink arcs runs from token 2 to token 9 (the [2,9] entry in the head-2 list), so it does not touch the first token. The caption contradicts the image.
  - *Fix:* Change [2,9] to an arc from token 0 (for example [0,9] or [0,3]), or delete it. Or reword the caption to 'one reaches back to the start and far back'.
- **kernel-engineering** (minor) — The caption says 'printed as a proof: keylines only, one tile inked', and the page's system notes say 'keylines only, one element inked in red'. In the render, the zoomed block (page x≈362–470, y≈509–617) is a solid shade (#1C2A69) fill behind its warp grid, which is not a keyline.
  - *Fix:* Remove the R(x2,y2,t,t,c.g2) fill so the block is keylines only, or reword the caption, e.g. 'keylines only, one block shaded, one tile inked'.
- **charlie-sound-room** (minor) — The red reply bars are centred on the lower lane rule, which is drawn in the jade shade (#4C9C6C, L*58). Red is L*46, only 12.6 L* away. This goes against the NOTES palette rule 'Red never touches a shade colour', which was the stated condition of the option-(a) greyscale fix for must-fix #4. On this cover the bars still separate from the field, so the must-fix itself holds. On 5d-parallelism the red edges also sit on the ultramarine shade hull (26 L* apart, so greyscale is not a problem there), which makes the NOTES claim untrue as written.
  - *Fix:* Draw the two lane rules in ink (or break the lower rule under the red run, x 392–600), so red only meets jade, ink or paper. Reword the NOTES rule to what the covers actually do, for example 'red never touches the intermediate shade'.
