# Art-director review — 03-pelican

**Score:** 7.5/10

**Verdict:** Halcyon is a disciplined system that is clearly Vizuara's own. The fixed band grid, the level coding (field colour, capsules and the word) and the 180px title legibility already hold the shelf together as one family, and most emblems are technically literate. The revision should fix one text collision, several trim-edge near-misses, a greyscale failure across the intermediate green, and two or three emblems that are pastiche or technically loose.

**Strongest:** neural-networks-from-scratch. It is one image of the real mechanism. Weight magnitude is wedge thickness, the sign is ink versus paper, the sigmoid sits inside the unit and the output leaves as one red signal. It bleeds decisively on both sides, so no edge looks like a trimming error. It reads instantly at 180px, the break 'Neural Networks / from Scratch' is clean, and it carries the print wrap well.

**Weakest:** ai-context-engineering. On book No. 01, the flagship, the emblem is Albers' Homage to the Square almost verbatim: five nested squares dropped by a fixed 0.74/0.26 top/bottom ratio (`top = prev.y + d*.74`). Nesting also says 'containment'. A context window is an ordered, finite sequence: the system prompt does not contain the tools, and the innermost square is not 'last'. The cover is strong as graphics but says the wrong thing about its subject.

## Must fix

1. **kernel-engineering** — The 84px two-line title plus the two-line 22px subtitle ends at y=332, and the black level band starts at y=336. 'GPU kernels for modern LLMs.' sits about 9px above the band. This is the only cover where text crowds the band; every other cover clears it by 34px or more.
   - *Fix:* Step the title down until the title block ends at least 24px above the band: at 74px it ends at about y=313. Add this to the fit routine: after the width check, if .tblock bottom > 312, drop the title one size.
2. **charlie-reasoning-room** — The lit red roof pane that encodes the volume number is the fourth bay's glazing at x=698–720, flush with the right trim. Normal trim drift (about ±6px) will shave the Vol. IV signal to a sliver. The fourth pane on I–III is also outside the 24px safe zone.
   - *Fix:* Offset the sawtooth by −60px (bays start at x=−60, 120, 300, 480, 660). The panes then land at x=98, 278, 458 and 638, all at least 24px inside the trim, and a fifth partial bay fills 660–720. Apply the same offset to all four Charlie covers so the roof stays identical.
3. **5d-parallelism** — The caption promises '32 GPUs at the corners of a 5-cube, all 80 edges drawn', but only 30 nodes are visible. With five directions 36° apart, vertex (+,−,+,−,+) and its complement land on the same point at the centre. The bottom apex (field y=560, field height 516) is off the page along with 5 of its edges. The two lower nodes at (200,508) and (520,508) touch the trim, and the top node is 8px under the band.
   - *Fix:* Set Rr=72 and cy=258, and give the red (expert) axis 0.8×Rr. I checked this: it gives 32 distinct nodes spanning field y 37–479, with at least 26px between nodes. The whole 5-cube then sits inside the field with about 37px clear of the band and trim, and the caption becomes true.
4. **build-llms-from-scratch / transformers-from-scratch / rag-in-production** — The NOTES say red separates from every field in greyscale, but on the intermediate covers it does not. Signal red is L*46, viridian L*58 and the viridian shade L*48. In a greyscale conversion the red next-token row on build-llms nearly vanishes into the field, and the red 'matching words' head on transformers turns into a faint mid-grey behind the ink arcs. On rag-in-production the three retrieved (red) books are the same grey as the dark-green shelf books, so only their lift shows retrieval. The red elements on charlie-vision-room and charlie-sound-room are also weak.
   - *Fix:* Choose one. (a) Lift the intermediate field to about #6DB98A (L*69, 23 L* from red), keep the shade near the current #4C9C6C, and never let red touch the shade (on RAG, recolour the dark-green books ink or paper). (b) Keep viridian and give every red mark on intermediate covers an ink ground: the LLM last row on an ink strip, the red arcs with a 3px ink casing. Re-run the greyscale contact sheet at 180px afterwards.
5. **pi-vs-hermes-vs-codex** — A 10px ink strip runs along the bottom trim (field y 506–516) and appears on no other cover. A 2.5mm band at the trim edge will come out anywhere from about 4 to 16px wide across a print run, and it looks like a registration error. The glyphs end 12px above it, so the bottom is cramped.
   - *Fix:* Delete the strip. If the glyphs need to stand on something, use the shared floor line proposed for the system and let it bleed at least 48px deep.
6. **build-llms-from-scratch** — The attention-sink column is a chain of colliding discs. Row 0 (r=27) and row 1 (r=22.3) overlap by about 8px at a 41px pitch, rows 1–2 overlap and rows 2–3 touch. That breaks the 'dot area = weight' encoding exactly where the caption points. The row-0 disc also sits 5.5px under the black band and reads as a blob hanging off it.
   - *Fix:* Cap the radius at 18px (at least a 5px gap at a 41px pitch) and scale the rest to match, or use N=11 with ch=44. Start the first row at field y 48 or lower so it clears the band by at least 24px. Row 0 is trivially weight 1 (it can only see itself); consider starting at token 2 so a meaningless dot doesn't dominate the sink column.
7. **charlie-vision-room** — The caption says 'the same patches, in reading order, on the belt', but the belt carries 7 blank paper squares for a 16-patch image. The disc content of the patches is lost, `seqc` is computed and never used, the grid stays complete, and the three 'travelling' patches start to the right of the grid rather than coming out of it. The belt tokens also float 10px above the belt, as they do 8px above it on charlie-sound-room, while on charlie-language-room they sit on it.
   - *Fix:* Put the patch tokens on the belt after the red CLS, each carrying its own crop of the ink disc (all 16 at about 26px, or the first 8 plus a gap). Leave holes in the grid where the three travelling patches came from, and route those patches from the grid down to the belt. Seat the tokens on the belt top (y=448) on all four Charlie covers.
8. **sql-masterclass** — The emblem draws a JOIN as the geometric overlap of two tables, with the red intersection as 'the join'. That is the Venn-diagram model of joins that SQL teachers specifically warn against: a join matches rows on a key and produces a wider table, not an intersection. It is especially bad on a beginner book. There is also a stray 20px paper sliver between table A's column rule (x≈284) and the red block (x=304).
   - *Fix:* Draw matching, not overlap. Set table A (paper) and table B (ink) side by side with their key columns in red. The rows whose keys match slide together into one wider result table below: A's columns plus B's columns, with a red key seam down the middle. Unmatched rows stay behind in the source tables (an inner join). This keeps the row rhythm and the paper/ink inversion.
9. **all renders** — All 16 JPGs are 720×889, not 720×888. Row 0 is page colour (#E4DED2) because each cover sits at a fractional y in the presentation page. Every thumbnail in the web library will carry a grey hairline on its top edge.
   - *Fix:* After fonts load, snap each figure so it starts on a whole pixel: `fig.style.marginTop = (Math.ceil(top) - top) + 'px'`, where top is the cover's getBoundingClientRect().top (no transform involved). Or give the .sec, .intro and figcaption text integer px line-heights. Re-render and confirm the JPGs come out 720×888.

## Improvements (highest impact first)

1. **system** — Write the emblem-field rule and apply it to every cover. Right now elements stop at arbitrary near-miss distances that look like trimming errors and make the lower edge of the shelf ragged. On mathematical-foundations the histogram ends 10px from the right trim and the matrix starts at x=40 against a 48px title margin. build-llms' last row ends 17px from the trim. decision-trees' disc is 16px from the left trim and its red square 20px from the right. sql-masterclass's lower table ends 20px from the bottom, deit's disc 22px and ai-context's outer square 18px. The kernel-engineering grid is off-centre (150px left vs 122px right; the 'zoom' in the code comment was never drawn). Grounded emblems also stand on five different floors (field y 432, 448, 452, 468, 506). The rule: every element either bleeds at least 12px past the trim or stays at least 48px inside it and at least 24px under the band, and every grounded composition stands on one shared floor (for example field y=468, page y=840).
2. **system** — Replace the seven title sizes (60/64/66/74/80/84/92, 2px steps nobody can see; 80 is not even in the scale in NOTES) with three steps chosen by rule: 92, 76 and 64. Pick the largest step whose widest line is at most 624px and whose title block ends at least 24px above the band, and make the fit routine choose the step instead of only shrinking. Examples: 'Neural Networks' is about 569px at 76, 'Build a Data-Efficient' about 590px at 64, 'Large Model Training' 593px at 64. Several titles are currently set well short of the measure (deit 552px at 60). For Charlie, 'Charlie and the / X Room' ends line 1 on an article and repeats 'Charlie' right under the kicker. Set 'Charlie and the' as a 30px line and make the room name alone the 76px hero ('Reasoning Room' is about 569px), so the four volumes differ in their largest type at 180px. 'Mathematical / Foundations / for Machine Learning' (597px at 64) breaks on sense better than 'Foundations for / Machine Learning'.
3. **ai-context-engineering** — Rethink the emblem away from the Albers nesting. In the same flat five-ink language, draw the context window as one fixed-width horizontal bar: an ink keyline from x=48 to 672 is the token budget. Fill it left to right in reading order: system prompt (ink), tool schemas (shade), retrieved passages (paper with text rules), history (the existing stripes), and the question as a red block hard against the right end. Stack the candidates that didn't fit (more passages, older turns) above the bar, with one or two being lifted in. This echoes the cover's own bands, makes 'engineering = choosing what fits the budget' visible, and keeps red last.
4. **charlie-sound-room** — Rethink the emblem. Two mirrored bar waveforms is the stock voice-assistant icon, and the paper frames on the belt have no relation to it. The specific problem a voice agent solves is turn-taking. Draw a two-lane timeline along the belt: your speech as paper bars on the upper lane, the agent's reply as red bars on the lower lane, the pause as an ink-bracketed gap (the endpointing and latency window), and a short overlap where you barge in and the red lane cuts off. Or stay inside the factory: slice the paper waveform into equal frames that ride the belt as spectrogram columns (stacked blocks for mel bins) into the same layer-stack machine as Vol. I, with the red reply waveform leaving it.
5. **system** — Redraw the device. At 44px on every cover the half-disc body with a flat bottom, round head and short bill reads as a duck on water, not a kingfisher. A bird sitting in a rounded cartouche is also the exact formula of the Penguin, Pelican and Puffin marks. Combined with the Marber-style band grid, the device is the one element that tips the direction from borrowing Penguin's method into borrowing its look. Keep the capsule and the construction from four flat shapes, but draw the kingfisher diving: a vertical dagger silhouette with the bill at least 40% of body length and the wings swept back, breaking out through the bottom of the capsule. It becomes more clearly a kingfisher and clearly Vizuara's own.
6. **system** — Before drawing the other 36 covers, add an emblem registry to NOTES and tighten the red rule. Two of 16 covers already share the 4×4 paper patch grid (charlie-vision-room, deit-from-scratch) and two draw attention (the LLM mask and the Transformers arcs). The unbuilt list has about 12 more transformer, vision and Kimi books, so patch grids and attention matrices would recur 8–10 times. Give each of the 52 books a diagram type (matrix, tree, stack, sequence, mesh, flow, projection, orbit…) and allow each type at most 3 times. Move DeiT off the patch grid, for example by drawing the CNN teacher as a pyramid of shrinking feature maps whose output lands on the red distillation token. Also enforce 'red = the one idea': decision-trees uses red for a whole class (three squares) and mathematical-foundations spreads it over three unrelated things. Allow one red element, or one red set, per cover.
