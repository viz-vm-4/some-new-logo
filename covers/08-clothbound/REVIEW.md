# Art-director review — 08-clothbound

**Score:** 7.5/10

**Verdict:** Buckram is the most distinctive and unified system on the table, and the shelf reads as one library with level visible at 180px, but four things have to be fixed before it can ship. A foil gradient fixed to the page dims the same part of every title and label. The print PDFs bake in a 96-ppi raster cloth. On the half-bound Charlie covers the running head runs into the corner fillet. And three emblems (Kernel, Reasoning Room, Language Room) are generic or muddled.

**Strongest:** 5d-parallelism. The Petrie-projected 5-cube is an honest emblem: five edge directions for five parallelism axes, and it is a 2×2×2×2×2 device mesh. Its silhouette is instantly memorable and stays crisp at 180px. Palladium on Ink (8.5:1 at the mid tone) gives the most legible title in the set, and the accelerator-mesh band ties it together. Runner-up: mathematical-foundations-for-ml, where the ellipse ratio (1/√3.7), the zig-zag caused by ηλ>1 and a band showing the gradient field of the same quadratic all come from one figure.

**Weakest:** ai-context-engineering. It is a core-set title and it has the weakest pairing: copper on Peacock measures 2.63:1 at the foil's mid tone and 1.19:1 at the dark stop. In the render the grey level of 'ENGINEERING' falls from ~214 to ~117 across the line (the cloth is ~71), so 'ERING' fades at 180px. The right half of the label sits only ~20 grey levels above the cloth, and in greyscale the title and the token-window device almost vanish into the teal.

## Must fix

1. **system (all covers; worst on ai-context-engineering, decision-trees-from-scratch, sql-masterclass, charlie-language-room, charlie-vision-room)** — Every foil element uses one 10-stop gradient fixed to the page (x1=-40,y1=0 to x2=760,y2=888). Its darkest stop (gold #8d6a25, copper #80401f, palladium #72777c, at t≈0.56) lands in the same place on every cover: the right end of title line 2 and the right half of the level/capsule label. Contrast of that stop against the cloth: Peacock/copper 1.19:1, Moss/gold 1.45:1, Madder/gold 1.51:1, Viridian/gold 1.63:1, Tobacco/gold 1.66:1. Visible results: 'CLASS' in SQL MASTERCLASS, 'TREES' and 'from Scratch' on Decision Trees and 'ERING' on AI Context all fade at 180px. 'TWENTY-THREE CAPSULES' on Decision Trees and '…Inference Engineering' / 'TWENTY-TWO CAPSULES' on Language Room are barely legible even at full size. The effect is worse in greyscale, and the same gradient is in the PDFs.
   - *Fix:* Give the type its own foil fill: a 3–4 stop gradient built only from stops at or above the mid tone (e.g. gold #c69c45 → #f3dc93 → #d7b565), set per text line with objectBoundingBox so every line gets the same highlight wherever it falls. Keep the full 10-stop sheen for the device, ring and fillets only. Add a build check that every stop used on type is at least 3:1 against the cloth for titles and at least 4.5:1 for the 13.5px label line.
2. **ai-context-engineering** — Even with the gradient fixed, copper on Peacock is the weakest cloth/foil pair in the set: 2.63:1 at copper's mid tone #bf7650. In the greyscale check the title, label and token-window device almost dissolve into the cloth. It is the only copper book, and it is a core-set title.
   - *Fix:* Re-foil in palladium (#b3b8bd, 4.67:1 on Peacock) or gold (#c69c45, 3.66:1). If copper must stay as the fourth foil, restrict it to its three lightest stops (#eba882/#fbd6bd/#f4c3a3, about 4.6:1) and pair it with a darker cloth than Peacock.
3. **charlie-vision-room, charlie-sound-room** — On the half-bound Charlie covers, the right-hand rule of the running head 'CHARLIE AND THE INTELLIGENCE FACTORY' ends at x≈632, y≈57.5. That is exactly where the gilt corner fillet (575,0 to 720,145) passes, so the two foil lines butt together (see the 2× crop of the top-right corner). The running head is centred on the board as if the corners were not there.
   - *Fix:* On half bindings, end the right rule at least 16px short of the fillet (end x = min(cx+wv/2+40, 575+57.5−16)) and shorten the left rule to match so the head stays symmetric. Alternatively drop the rules to 20px on half-bound covers, or set the running head 10px lower with shorter rules.
4. **system (renders/pdf/*.pdf)** — The print PDFs are not the clean vector print file NOTES describes. NOTES says the texture layers are not in the print file, but no '.po' layer exists in the code; only the '.so' shadows, speck and weave are hidden for print. Each PDF embeds the cloth texture as a 720×888 raster stretched over 7.5×9.25in, which is 96 ppi. It also embeds the leather grain at 360×1776 (≈192 ppi) and bakes in the light, vignette, hinge and lap shading. A POD paperback printed from these files would be a low-res photo of a fake hardcover, which is the 'raster that prints badly' problem the brief rejects.
   - *Fix:* Put class 'so' on the cloth <image>, the leather <image>, and the light, vig, hinge and lap rects. In @media print, output flat cloth colour, a flat black strip and corners, flat foil (a single Pantone 871/876/877 value, no sheen gradient) and the band as a ~25% darker flat tint, so the PDF is a pure-vector paperback/POD file. If any texture must print, generate it at ≥300 ppi (2250×2775). For the case-bound edition, also export the foil die and the blind die as separate one-colour vector plates.
5. **pi-vs-hermes-vs-codex** — Please verify this one with the author; I can't confirm it without the book. As drawn, the triptych probably misstates two of the three strategies. The Codex column shows evicted (dashed) history plus recent lines with no summary, but Codex CLI's /compact and auto-compact write a handoff summary and keep recent user messages. Hermes Agent's context compressor, as publicly documented, summarises the middle turns and protects the first and last ones; that is a sandwich, not the 'progressive compression' drawn. Separately, the code comment in triptych() gives a different column mapping from NOTES, which suggests the assignment was changed during iteration.
   - *Fix:* Check the three mechanisms against the book's nine capsules and redraw each column as its actual strategy, keeping the triptych and plinth. For example: Pi = summary block over recent lines; Hermes = kept head lines, a summary block, kept tail lines; Codex = summary block plus recent user turns only, with tool output dropped as dashes. Update NOTES and the code comment to match.

## Improvements (highest impact first)

1. **system** — Write assignment rules now, so the shelf can grow to 50 books without repeats reading as random. At present 14 cloths and 4 foils are handed out ad hoc (copper appears only on AI Context, black pigment only on Saffron). Tie cloth to subject family, for example: maths and foundations = Prussian/Cobalt; neural nets and DL = Bottle/Viridian/Moss; LLMs and language = Oxblood/Madder/Claret; systems and infra = Ink/Slate + palladium; agents and context = Peacock/Saffron. Make gold the default foil and every other foil mean something. Also cap the 'grid of squares' device: DeiT (a 4×4 patch grid) and Kernel (a 4×4 SM grid) are near-twins side by side on the 180px sheet, and Language Room, the Vision iris and the patches/mesh/stairs bands draw on the same vocabulary. CNN, ViT, NanoVLM and Transformers for Vision are all still to come. Rule: every device must have a unique silhouette at ~45px, which is the roundel's size in a thumbnail.
2. **system** — Make the level code ordinal. At present beginner = strip (one leather piece), intermediate = strip + corners (two pieces) and advanced = plain full cloth (none), so what you see goes 1 → 2 → 0. A bookbinder will also read it backwards, because full cloth is the plainest trade binding and half leather the grandest. Swap to beginner = full cloth, intermediate = quarter-bound, advanced = half-bound: visible leather goes 0 → 1 → 2 and follows binding prestige. As a bonus, all four Charlie volumes would then carry the spine strip (I and IV half, II and III quarter), so the set matches in a slipcase instead of alternating full/half/half/full.
3. **kernel-engineering** — The weakest emblem. A package with pins and an SM grid is the stock 'AI chip' icon. It mirrors DeiT's 4×4 grid, and blind-blocked at 1.4:1 it is an empty roundel at 180px. Replace it with the diagram every kernel engineer draws, tiled GEMM: A to the left, B above, C in the classic L layout, with one output tile of C solid and the row panel of A and column panel of B that feed it hatched. It is exactly what a kernel does (stage tiles through shared memory), and its silhouette differs from every other device. Keep the forthcoming-blind rule, but deepen the blind tone to at least 2:1, or foil only the outline of the C tile, so the book isn't blank in the web library.
4. **charlie-reasoning-room** — At 180px the search tree reads as a candelabrum with stray 'W' and 'x' marks. The pruned crosses sit on neighbouring branches, and the goal ring sits only ~5px under the IV rule. Rethink it as a sampled group (GRPO, the RL behind reasoning models): one prompt node at the base of the arch, six straight rays of different lengths rising as sampled chains, each ending in a reward dot (solid = correct, open = wrong), and the best chain in heavy foil. To keep 'from bandits', make the first fork three short arms with pull-knobs. It fills the arch, keeps the reading at thumbnail size, and needs no crosses.
5. **charlie-language-room** — The prefill-then-decode column is ~90px wide in a 262px arch. It reads as a thermometer, and the knock-outs chop its thin spine into dashes. Replace it with continuous batching, the core idea of inference engineering: 5–6 horizontal request lanes stacked in the arch, each a wide prefill bar followed by a run of decode squares of different lengths. Stagger the lanes so a new request's prefill slots into a row another request has just freed. It fills the arch, stays true to the subject, and repeats nothing else in the set.
6. **system** — Typography pass. (1) Charlie subtitles repeat the room word: 'VISION ROOM / Vision — Vision Transformers' has 'Vision' three times in two lines. Drop the prefix and set 'Vision Transformers', 'Inference Engineering', 'Voice Agents', 'Reinforcement Learning, from bandits to reasoning models'. (2) The title block is centred in the 66–392 zone, so the label line drifts from y≈308 (AI Context, 110px of dead cloth above the band) to y≈383 (Charlie, 35px). Fix the ornament and label to one baseline (e.g. 372) and stack the title upward from it, so the title/band junction is identical across the shelf. (3) 5d-parallelism: '5D' sits as a small stranded first line; scale it like SQL (scale:[1.7,1]) so the numeral carries the cover. (4) Drop the full stop after 'LLMs.' in the Kernel subtitle. (5) Nudge the Reasoning Room device down ~10px so the goal ring clears the numeral rule. (6) Raise the Neural Networks edge weights from 1.4px to your own stated 1.5px foil minimum.
