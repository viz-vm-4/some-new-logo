Vizuara Books · Cover system proposal 08

BuckramThe Vizuara bindery edition: every book bound in cloth, blocked in one foil, pressed with one blind die

A technical library should feel like something you keep. So every Vizuara book is designed as a real case-bound hardcover: a deep bookcloth, a single foil and a single blind die. The title is blocked in foil at the head of the board. Below it, in a sunk roundel, is the book's own device, drawn from the mechanism the book teaches: a causal mask, a penteract, a gradient descent through its contours. A blind-blocked band repeats that subject as texture, in the way a clothbound classic repeats its motif.

You can read the level from the binding itself. Beginner books are quarter-bound, intermediate books are half-bound (spine and corners) and advanced books are in full cloth, so a book with more binding goes deeper. The Vizuara mark is a small stamped device at the tail of the board: an open book seen end-on, which is also a V.

How it prints

ClothReal bookcloth (a buckram or linen-finish cloth) matched to the swatch. The weave, mottle and lighting on screen only stand in for it and are never printed.
FoilHot-foil blocking from one brass die per book, in gold, copper, palladium or black pigment foil. Every foil line is at least 1.5 px (1.1 pt), and counters are open.
Blind bandA second brass die pressed with no foil (blind blocking). The pressed cloth darkens and burnishes, which is the darker tone shown here.
RoundelA sunk panel made by a deeper, two-level die: the roundel is pressed about 0.6 mm, and the foil device is blocked into it.
Quarter / halfA two-cloth case. The spine and corners are in house black leather-grain cloth, with a gilt fillet blocked along each join.
Paperback / PODPrinted as flat colour. The foil becomes Pantone 871 / 876 / 877 C metallic (or a spot-gloss UV over a flat tone), and the blind band becomes a 25% darker tint under matt laminate. The PDFs are this proof: the foil, blind and type are vector, and only the cloth texture is raster.
