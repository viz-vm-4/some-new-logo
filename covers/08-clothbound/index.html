Vizuara Books · Cover system proposal 08

BuckramThe Vizuara bindery edition: every book bound in cloth, blocked in one foil, pressed with one blind die

A technical library should feel like something you keep. So every Vizuara book is designed as a real case-bound hardcover: a deep bookcloth, a single foil and a single blind die. The title is blocked in foil at the head of the board. Below it, in a sunk roundel, is the book's own device, drawn from the mechanism the book teaches: a causal mask, a penteract, a gradient descent through its contours. A blind-blocked band repeats that subject as texture, in the way a clothbound classic repeats its motif.

You can read the level from the binding itself. Beginner books are in full cloth, intermediate books are quarter-bound (a leather-grain spine) and advanced books are half-bound (spine and corners). The leather you can see goes 0, 1, 2 as the book goes deeper, in the same order as binding prestige. The Vizuara mark is a small stamped device at the tail of the board: an open book seen end-on, which is also a V.

Assignment rules

  1. Cloth follows the subject family (above). Within a family, neighbouring books alternate cloths, so fifty books stay a library rather than a random spread.
  2. Gold is the default foil, and every other foil means something: palladium for systems and infrastructure, copper for agents, context and memory, black pigment only on a light cloth (Saffron). A series keeps one foil across its volumes.
  3. Level is the binding: full cloth, quarter or half. It is never a colour.
  1. Each device has a unique silhouette at 45 px (the size of the roundel in a 180 px thumbnail). The device draws the book's mechanism, not a generic icon, and it may not repeat another device's figure.
  2. Type is legible by construction. Lettering uses only the light stops of its foil, so each line gets the same highlight wherever it falls. A build check requires every type stop to reach at least 3:1 against the cloth, and the label and print foil at least 4.5:1 (table below).
  3. Forthcoming books carry their device blind, with only its key element foiled, until publication.

NEURAL NETWORKS

Title: Cormorant Garamond Bold in capitals. The strokes are heavy enough to take foil on cloth without closing up, and the serifs are sharp enough to keep the stamp crisp.

from Scratch

Continuations: Semibold Italic. Long titles are broken the way a title page is, with the substance in capitals and the connecting words in italic, so the capitals never run past three lines.

BEGINNER · THIRTY-THREE CAPSULES

Labels are spaced capitals at 10 pt, always on the same baseline (372). Capsule counts are written out in words, as an old binding would give its volume count.

How it prints

ClothReal bookcloth (a buckram or linen-finish cloth) matched to the swatch. The weave, mottle, grain and lighting are screen-only and never reach the print file.
FoilHot-foil blocking from one brass die per book, in gold, copper, palladium or black pigment foil. Every foil line is at least 1.5 px (1.1 pt), and counters are open.
Blind bandA second brass die pressed with no foil (blind blocking). The pressed cloth darkens and burnishes, which is the darker tone shown here.
RoundelA sunk panel made by a deeper, two-level die: the roundel is pressed about 0.6 mm, and the foil device is blocked into it.
Quarter / halfA two-cloth case. The spine and corners are in house black leather-grain cloth, with a gilt fillet blocked along each join.
Paperback / PODThe PDFs are this file: flat cloth colour, the blind band as a 28% darker flat tint, and every foil element in one flat value, to print as Pantone 871 / 876 / 877 C metallic or as a spot-gloss UV over a flat tone. It is pure vector with no images, so it prints sharp at any size.
