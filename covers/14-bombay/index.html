Direction 14 · Vizuara Books cover system

Signboard. Every Vizuara book is lettered like a Bombay shop board and labelled like a Sivakasi matchbox: one bespoke, hand-built display letter — outline, inline, split-colour fill and block shadow — painted on a flat enamel ground, with a printed label that carries the one object the book is about.

The street supplies the craft, the publisher supplies the discipline. The board colour tells you the shelf, the perforated stamp tells you the level, the label tells you the idea, and the imprint signs off quietly at the foot — in Latin and Devanagari at equal size, the way Maharashtra's shop-sign rule asks Marathi to stand beside English.

Enamel palette

Board colour = shelf

Seven tins, one per part of the library: Chrome Foundations · Signal Red Neural Networks · Maroon Language Models · Bottle Green Agents & Context · Post Blue Systems & Scale · Firozi Vision & Robotics · Coal for the Charlie sub-series.

Letters are always Chuna over Chrome with a Signal Red inline and a coal keyline; the yellow shelf inverts to red letters.

Level = the stamp

A perforated stamp pasted on the label's corner. It heats up with difficulty: 1 Chrome (Beginner), 2 Signal Red (Intermediate), 3 Coal (Advanced). Legible at 180px; survives greyscale because the three fields are light, mid and dark.

Grid

24px safe edge, painted double rule at 16/25px, title board 58–436, label 96–624 × 470–788, imprint baseline 842.
