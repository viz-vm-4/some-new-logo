# Art-director review — 14-bombay

**Score:** 8/10

**Verdict:** Signboard is an ownable, rigorously built system: the lettering is drawn in code, the grid is fixed, and the shelf and level logic survives 180px and greyscale. It is held back by script connectives that collide with the block letters and by several emblems that are either busy pipelines or get their own mechanism slightly wrong.

**Strongest:** 5d-parallelism. The title "5D" is a 150px numeral, so the cover works as a sign at any size. The device mesh is one isometric object on the ray centre. It names all five axes correctly: data, tensor and pipeline as solid axes, expert and context as dashed run-offs. It still reads at 180px. It is the clearest example of the system's own rule (a name panel above one central object), and rag-in-production is a close second.

**Weakest:** charlie-sound-room. The caption says "THE VOICE LOOP", but the drawing is a one-way chain (waveform → spectrogram → horn) with nothing closing it and no model in it, so it shows audio playback, not a voice agent. The spectrogram's top harmonics also run off the box and come back in as white slashes, and the horn's third sound arc hits the panel frame.

## Must fix

1. **mathematical-foundations-for-ml, 5d-parallelism, vla-world-models, pi-vs-hermes-vs-codex, neural-networks-from-scratch, build-llms-from-scratch (mixLine)** — The Yellowtail connectives run into the Board Block letter that follows them. 'for' touches the M keyline (Math) and the L (5D). On VLA, '&' touches the W and 'for' touches the R. On NN and LLMs, 'from' is about 3px from the S. On Pi, 'and' is set tiny and low, so the trade line reads 'COMPACTIONandMEMORY'. The cause is in mixLine: x advances by textW, the script's advance width, plus 0.16H. Yellowtail's italic terminals (r, s, d, &) hang past that advance.
   - *Fix:* In mixLine, after a script item add an overhang allowance of about 0.18×size, then use the same 0.24H gap that separates block words. For Pi's trade line, give 'and' k≈1.25 and base≈0.8 so it spans the cap band, with at least 8px clear on each side. Then re-check all seven lines at 2x.
2. **sql-masterclass** — The inner-join emblem is wrong. The key-cell dash length comes from (i+j)%3 separately in each table, so two of the four wires join different keys: left row 3 (short dash) goes to right row 4 (medium), and left row 4 (long) goes to right row 2 (short). Every row also has a match, so the drawing is a plain permutation and cannot be told apart from an outer join. The INNER part is not shown.
   - *Fix:* Take each key glyph from a key id shared by the pair, for example 1–4 pips or a dash length tied to the id, so wired rows show identical keys. Add one row with no partner to each table. Knock those rows back the way the causal mask's future cells are (chuna fill plus a strike) and give them no wire.
3. **pi-vs-hermes-vs-codex** — The compaction press looks like it expands its input. The pressed block (about 140×96px) has more area than the 11-slip history that went in (about 62×176px), so the one idea the emblem exists for reads backwards.
   - *Fix:* Make the history a tall column clipped off the top of the panel so it reads as 'too long'. Press it into a brick of at most a third of that area, about 90×34, keeping the same stripe colours squeezed thin, and file that small brick in the drawer. The freed space lets the press itself get bigger.
4. **charlie-language-room** — The autoregressive feedback arc starts at the far right of the belt, which is the oldest token. The red, just-stamped token sits at the machine's outlet, so the loop labelled 'NEXT TOKEN' feeds back the wrong token.
   - *Fix:* Start the dashed arc at the red token (about x = mx+mw+45) and bring it up over the machine into its top. The alternative is to run the belt so the newest token is at the right end and red. Change the tag to 'FED BACK' or 'APPEND'.
5. **charlie-sound-room** — Spectrogram harmonics 6 and 7 fall outside the box: yc = gy+gh-6-k·f0·0.92 reaches about 161px in a 124px box. They are clipped and come back in as near-vertical white slashes at the top left, which reads as a rendering glitch. The third sound arc from the horn also runs into the panel's right frame.
   - *Fix:* Scale so that 7·max(f0)·s ≤ gh−12 (s≈0.6), or draw 5 harmonics. Pull the horn 20px left or drop to two arcs so the outermost clears the frame by at least 10px.
6. **mathematical-foundations-for-ml** — The flag labelled as the minimum is planted at cx+26. The parabola's minimum is at cx and the descent steps stop at cx−5, so in a maths book the flag marks a point that is not the minimum.
   - *Fix:* Plant the pole at (cx, f(cx)) and end the last step at cx. Fly the pennant left, or shorten it, if it collides with the last ball.
7. **kernel-engineering** — The dashed A→SRAM curve is drawn after the 'A' matrix label and runs straight through it. It starts inside A at row 2.5 and exits left across the letter, which at full size reads as a broken '!'-like glyph.
   - *Fix:* Start the curve at the top edge of A's red row (axg+M/2, cyg+2c) and route it up, or move the A label under its grid. Draw all A/B/C labels after the paths.
8. **deit-from-scratch** — The (DeiT) cartouche uses about 18px text, which is roughly 4px at 180px. The acronym readers actually search for disappears in the library grid.
   - *Fix:* Set DEIT as a Board Block trade word at 0.7× or more of the 'from SCRATCH' cap height, or enlarge the tag text to H×0.62 and move it to the end of the TRANSFORMER line.
9. **system (level stamp)** — 'INTERMEDIATE' is set at 8.8px (6.6pt at trim), reversed out of Signal Red and letterspaced. That is below a safe size for reversed type and will fill in on a CMYK red build. BEGINNER and ADVANCED are at 10.5px, so the stamp is also inconsistent.
   - *Fix:* Set all three level words at 10.5px or more in Anek Latin wdth 75 with about 0.6px tracking, widening the stamp field from 68 to 72px if needed. Keep the numeral as it is.

## Improvements (highest impact first)

1. **system** — Tighten the emblem grammar before drawing the other 35 emblems. Nine of the 15 emblems are left-to-right A→B→C pipelines with small arrows and paper tags: context window, press, card catalogue, decode loop, patch grid, voice, distillation, arm, tiled matmul. At 180px they become rows of crumbs and the sunburst centres on nothing, which breaks your own Sivakasi principle of one central object. The single objects (device mesh, causal mask, gradient bowl, bandits) are the ones that read at thumbnail. Write the rule into NOTES: one object covering at least 50% of the panel, on the ray centre, at most one arrow and two tags. For example, RAG's drawer with three cards up is enough, and the query and prompt stacks can go. Also stop reusing the pile of coloured slips: ai-context-engineering and pi-vs-hermes-vs-codex sit on the same shelf and both open with the same stack.
2. **build-llms-from-scratch** — 'BUILD LARGE / LANGUAGE / MODELS' splits the noun phrase, so 'BUILD LARGE' reads as a phrase of its own. Re-break it as: BUILD as a small trade line (tr, H≈34), then 'LARGE LANGUAGE' at H≈78 (14 characters fit the 612 measure at r≈0.56), then 'MODELS (LLMs)', then 'from SCRATCH'.
3. **charlie-sound-room** — Rethink the emblem as a real loop centred on the rays: a ring with the listener/mic at 9 o'clock, a transcript slip at 12, a reply slip at 3 and the horn at 6. The horn's sound arcs curl back into the mic. Put one red notch on the ring at the turn-taking (VAD) point where a user can barge in. That shows listen → think → speak → listen, which is what a voice agent is and what the caption promises.
4. **vla-world-models** — Rethink the emblem. It has seven parts, and the 16px dashed paper stroke reads as a candy-striped arc rather than a ghost arm; the camera sightlines also cut through the arm's base. Keep one solid arm reaching for the block. Draw the world model's rollout as three outline-only copies of the block and gripper (t+1, t+2, t+3), rising along the lift path in a 3px coal keyline on paper, with a single 'PREDICTED' tag. Drop the camera, and put the instruction on one tag on the base.
5. **neural-networks-from-scratch** — The 3-4-4-2 node-and-edge graph is the stock image for every neural-network book, and the 22px activation squiggles disappear at 180px. For 'from scratch', draw one neuron opened up and large: three input wires whose line weights are the weights, a Σ drum, a bias knob, an S-curve window and one output wire. It is a single object that fills the ray centre. Deep Learning Fundamentals can then take the stacked-layer version without repeating it.
6. **charlie-* (sub-series)** — Each Charlie cover says CHARLIE AND THE twice (series line and lockup) and INTELLIGENCE FACTORY twice (series line and foot). Shrink the top line to a small 'BOOK I OF IV' cartouche and let the foot carry the series name. This also lets the title block start at 58 like every other cover instead of 86, so title tops line up across the shelf.
