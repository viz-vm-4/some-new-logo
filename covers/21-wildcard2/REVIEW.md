# Art-director review — 21-wildcard2

**Score:** 8/10

**Verdict:** This is an ownable, witty system: the ML model becomes a scale model, and a sprue on a true-inch cutting mat is built by a real layout engine. The type is disciplined and every title reads at 180px. Four slips need fixing before it ships: a stamp collision, a few technical errors in the emblems, a level mark that nearly disappears in the thumbnails, and a maker's tab whose position drifts from cover to cover.

**Strongest:** ai-context-engineering. The hero part is a compartmented context tray labelled SYSTEM / TOOLS / RETRIEVED / HISTORY, with the document, message, tool, memory chip and retriever loose on the sprue beside it. That is the book's argument turned into an object: "what goes into the window, and what stays out". The metaphor does real work here instead of decorating. The lilac tray reads instantly at 180px, and the title-over-tagline-over-sprue hierarchy is textbook. build-llms-from-scratch, with a correctly oriented causal-mask triangle and to|ken|s, is a close second.

**Weakest:** charlie-sound-room. Apart from the waveform hero, the sprue is a UI icon set: a microphone, two copies of the generic message bubble (the same part already used on ai-context-engineering and charlie-language-room) and a speaker with sound arcs. Nothing on it shows how a voice agent works: no speech recognition (ASR), language model and speech synthesis (TTS) loop, no voice-activity detection (VAD), no turn-taking, no latency budget. Of the 16 covers this is the closest to the clip-art the brief set out to kill.

## Must fix

1. **kernel-engineering** — The TEST SHOT / COMING SOON stamp sits right on the top runner. Part 2's gate and its numbered tag show through the stamp's letters, so at full size it reads "TES[2]T SHOT" (checked in the 2x PNG). The `reserve` option only moves the maker's tab. Gate tags are still placed under the stamp.
   - *Fix:* Keep the stamp off the runners. Either (a) add the stamp's bounding box to `runners[0].occ` and `.block` before gates and tags are placed, so tag 2 flips to the left of its gate and part 2 shifts clear, or (b) move the stamp into the empty bench area to the right of the tagline (around x 470–672, y 290–330). If it must overlap plastic, give it an opaque bench-coloured knockout plate so nothing shows through the lettering.
2. **rlhf-from-scratch** — Part 4 is named "Clipped objective" (and NOTES calls it "the PPO clipped objective"), but `clipFn()` draws a flat–ramp–flat curve. That is clip(r, 1−ε, 1+ε) itself, not PPO's L^CLIP = min(r·A, clip(r)·A). L^CLIP has only one flat segment: for A>0 it rises and then flattens at 1+ε, and it has no lower floor. Anyone who has read the PPO paper will catch this.
   - *Fix:* Redraw the curve as the A>0 case: a straight rise from the lower-left to r = 1+ε, then flat (e.g. `M12 80L104 24H148`), with a small engraved tick on the axis at 1+ε and optionally at 1. Otherwise rename the part "clip(r, 1−ε, 1+ε)" in the figcaption and NOTES.
3. **decision-trees-from-scratch** — The two decision-tree parts contradict each other. The tree splits x ≤ 2.5, then y ≤ 1 on the left and x ≤ 4 on the right, which gives a horizontal step plus a vertical band. The boundary tile draws a two-step staircase. Two filled class dots, `[18,74]` and `[28,62]` in `boundary()`, also sit on the hollow side of the lower step (visible at around 130,690 and 143,677 in the render). On a book about building trees, the tree and its boundary should agree.
   - *Fix:* Change the right child's label from 'x ≤ 4' to 'y ≤ 4'. The tree then produces exactly the staircase drawn (a low step left of 2.5, a high step right of it). Move the two stray filled dots below the lower step (e.g. to [16,104] and [44,104]) or make them hollow.
4. **mathematical-foundations-for-ml** — The Gaussian is advertised as having "σ ticks", but `bell()` computes exp(−(d/30)²). That makes σ ≈ 21px, so the ticks at ±30 and ±60 land at about 1.41σ and 2.83σ. The curve's inflection points visibly fall inside the first ticks. This is a factual error on the maths book.
   - *Fix:* Use exp(−d²/(2·30²)), i.e. `Math.exp(-((x-w/2)**2)/(2*30*30))`, and widen w to about 220 so the tails reach near zero before the part's edge. The ticks at ±30 and ±60 then fall on ±1σ and ±2σ, and the inflection points sit on the inner ticks.
5. **deit-from-scratch** — The hero misrepresents DeiT and renders badly. Both the teacher and the student are drawn as MLPs, but DeiT distils a convnet teacher into a transformer student, and that student is the book's subject. The teacher is `network([3,5,5,3])` at r=13, dx=66, and its 5×5 middle rods fuse into a solid red mass (see the 2x PNG). At 180px it is a blob. The image-patch engraving is also fragmentary: the mountain lines break at every tile gap, and the sun is a clipped arc in one corner.
   - *Fix:* Draw the teacher as a CNN: 3–4 stacked feature-map slabs shrinking in size, engraved 'CNN'. Make the student a token row [CLS][p][p][p][DIST] feeding an encoder block (reuse `encoder()`). Place the DIST tile next to the teacher so the eye reads teacher → DIST. If you keep a network part, go no denser than [3,4,3] with dx ≥ 80. For `patchGrid()`, fill the mountain as a raised relief area (a solid polygon clipped to the tiles) instead of a 2.2px engraved line, so the picture survives the gaps. This also fixes charlie-vision-room.

## Improvements (highest impact first)

1. **system** — Make skill level readable at 180px. The brief requires level to be encoded, but at 180px each level square is about 5px and the "SKILL · …" label is about 2px. Telling 2 filled squares from 3 means comparing one 5px square, and I could barely do it on the contact sheet. Enlarge `levelRow` squares from 21px to about 32px (gap 8, no digits needed) and keep the label beside them. Or repeat the level as 1–3 molded pips on the maker's tab, which is plastic-coloured and much more visible. Re-check with tools/contact.js.
2. **system** — Lock the maker's tab to one slot. `scan()` puts it wherever there is room: top-right on ai-context, math, trees, SQL, git, vision and sound; top-centre on NN, 5D, DeiT, RLHF, language and reasoning; bottom-centre on build-llms and kernel. On pi-vs-hermes-vs-codex it falls back to a 44px stub with no VIZUARA at all. The tab is one of only two brand placements. Reserve x 530–646 on the top runner for every cover, push it into `runners[0].block` before any part is laid out, and shift parts to fit around it rather than moving the tab.
3. **charlie-sound-room** — Keep the waveform hero. Replace the mic / bubble / bubble / speaker row with parts that show the voice-agent mechanism. 1) A loop of three molded stations engraved ASR → LLM → TTS, with a short barge-in arrow cutting back across it. 2) A VAD part: a short waveform with a bracket window molded over the speech section. 3) A turn-taking timeline: two parallel lanes, USER and AGENT, with offset segments that overlap slightly. 4) Optionally a latency bar engraved '< 800 ms'. All of these are simple rods and tiles in the existing parts vocabulary, and none of them reuses the message bubble.
4. **5d-parallelism** — The five identical upward arrows labelled DP / TP / PP / CP / EP say "five labels", not "five dimensions", and all pointing the same way undercuts the title. The 4×4 chip grid hero is also the same waffle as kernel-engineering's tile grid. Replace the arrows with one axis-hub part: five rods radiating at 72° from a hub, each ending in a DP/TP/PP/CP/EP tile. Make it the hero next to the all-reduce ring. Mold the ring's direction chevrons onto the ring segments instead of floating 5px engravings inside it. Move the chip grid and pipeline stages to row 2. While there, reset the title as 'Build'-style two lines, '5D Parallelism for / Large Model Training', so the sprue gains about 70px and the last line isn't a lone word.
5. **pi-vs-hermes-vs-codex** — The C-clamp compacting a block is a great part, but the 'Long transcript' next to it reads as a fishbone or comb: pills skewered on a rod, with the gate running straight through it. Enclose it as one long rounded strip, a tall tray with alternating left and right message pills molded inside, sized so it is visibly about three times the height of the clamped block. The before/after then reads at 180px. The Hermes badge's wing reads as a leaf. Redraw it as a winged petasos (helmet) silhouette or a three-feather wing with clear primaries. If the book has distinct compaction strategies per agent, show those instead of the badges.
6. **system** — Plan for 50 books now. Among 16 covers the message bubble already appears 4 times, `network()` 4 times, `patchGrid()` twice, and the chip grid, memory chip and ATTN block twice each. About 15 transformer and LLM titles will all reach for the causal mask, QKV and tokens. Add a hero registry to the engine: a hero part is used once in the catalogue, and shared parts may appear only in row 2. Every cover also uses the same two-row frame with a hero scale cap of 1.3. Allow single-row 'big part' kits and a higher cap (about 1.6) for the hero so the grid of 50 has rhythm. Also tidy the type: 'Build Large Language / Models (LLMs)' splits the noun phrase, so reset it as 'Build Large / Language Models / (LLMs) from Scratch'. On the four Charlie covers, set 'Charlie and the' as a smaller series line so the room name, which is what tells them apart at 180px, becomes the biggest word.
