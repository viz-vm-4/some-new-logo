Some Assembly Required

Every Vizuara book builds a model. So every cover is a model kit.

The subject of each book is broken into its real components (the causal mask, the all-reduce ring, the KV cache, the split test) and molded onto a plastic sprue, numbered in build order and ready to snap off. It is the honest picture of a library whose titles keep saying from scratch: here are the pieces, now go and put them together. The pun does half the work: an ML model, a scale model.

The bench, the frame, the type and the imprint never change; only the parts do, so fifty covers read as one shelf and no two look alike. The plastic’s colour names the field. Skill level is printed the way kit makers always have: Skill 1, 2 or 3. Everything is vector SVG drawn in code, from the injection-moulded highlights to the cutting mat, which is ruled in true inches at trim size.

    Skill level

    Three numbered squares, filled to the level, like the skill badge on a kit box. The fill count reads at thumbnail size and in greyscale.

    Imprint

    A V molded on its own little sprue. On covers it sits small, top-left; VIZUARA is also molded into every sprue’s maker’s tab, as real manufacturers do.

    The rules

    1. One bench. Every cover sits on the same cutting mat, ruled in true inches along the left and bottom edges. Printed at 7.5 × 9.25 in, the ruler is accurate.
    2. Title first. Cream Archivo ExtraBold, top-left, 66–104 px, always the loudest thing. A one-line truthful tagline in Martian Mono sits under it.
    3. One sprue per book: the emblem. Three to eight parts taken from the book’s actual mechanism, numbered in build order. One hero part is big enough to carry the 180 px thumbnail.
    4. Brand signs, title speaks. A small imprint top-left, and VIZUARA plus the sprue letter molded into the maker’s tab.
    5. Colour has a job. The plastic names the subject family (seven plastics). Numbered squares give the level. The footer carries capsules and hours. The kit number, top-right, is the catalogue number, so the library can be collected in order.
    6. Special runs. Charlie and the Intelligence Factory is the Factory Edition: gold-plated sprues on a chocolate bench, with Roman sprue letters I–IV. Unreleased books ship as a Test Shot: clear, unpainted plastic with a stamp.
    7. Scales to the catalogue. A layout engine places the parts, cuts the gates, numbers the tags and finds a clear spot for the maker’s tab, so a new book only needs its parts list.
    8. Survives print. Pure vector, with no rule under 1 px at trim. All plastics are light on a dark bench, so everything holds in greyscale.

    The core six

    Range: Skill 1 to Test Shot

    A four-part beginner kit, a two-word title at 104 px, a 55-character title in three lines, an advanced alignment kit, a book that is not about AI at all, and an unreleased book as a clear test shot.

    Factory Edition: Charlie and the Intelligence Factory

    Four rooms, one gold-plated set. The Factory Edition keeps every rule of the system and changes only the bench (chocolate) and the finish (gold plate), so the sub-series pops off the shelf and still belongs to it.

    Print wrap: back, spine, front

    The back cover works like the side panel of a kit box: the same parts, out of the sprue and assembled, drawn like an instruction sheet. The spine carries the plastic colour and the kit number, so a shelf of Vizuara books reads as a numbered, colour-coded set.
