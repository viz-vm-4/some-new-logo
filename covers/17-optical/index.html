Vizuara Books · cover system · direction 17

Inter­ference

Every Vizuara book is one field of black lines and one spot colour. The book's idea is the force that bends the lines — a Gaussian swells them, a causal mask turns them, compaction squeezes them, attention makes them interfere.

Built like op art (Riley, Vasarely, Cruz-Diez): from across the room each field averages into a bold tonal emblem; in the hand it starts to move. Level is the grain of the line — beginner fields are cut coarse, advanced ones fine — so the shelf sorts itself by texture.
