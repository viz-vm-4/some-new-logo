# Art-director review — 01-grid

**Score:** 8/10

**Verdict:** This is a disciplined, well-executed Swiss programme. Using the ground ink to show level works well at 180px and in greyscale, and most figures are the real mechanism of their book. The revision round should fix a handful of collisions and clipping artefacts, two technically muddled figures (5D, diffusion), a stale render that would ship to the gallery, and a creeping sameness among the square-matrix figures.

**Strongest:** build-llms-from-scratch. The figure is the actual mechanism, not a picture of the topic: a lower-triangular causal attention matrix with the upper triangle masked, a heavier first column (a nice, true attention-sink touch), the diagonal dominant, and the last row, the token being predicted, in the signal ink. It sits exactly on the module grid, reads at 180px as a clean staircase, and the print wrap's back cover explains it in one sentence. That is the system working as intended. neural-networks-from-scratch is a close second: nine inputs weighted by |w| and a ReLU-bent output, with a bold silhouette that holds at thumbnail size.

**Weakest:** vla-world-models. It is the only pictorial figure in the set: a drawing of a robot arm, which breaks the programme's own Rule 4 ("the mechanism of the book, not a picture of its topic"). It is also the only figure built from free diagonals instead of the grid. The outlined 'imagined' gripper, drawn as 8px bars with a 4px stroke, clots into a triple-line comb at full size and is mush at 180px. The dotted trajectory runs straight through the imagined arm's wrist ring and into its gripper. The 'Vision-Language' half of the title is not represented at all. Together with the smallest title on the shelf (46px), it looks like it came from a different, more illustrative system.

## Must fix

1. **git-v** — A stale render of an abandoned variant, renders/git-v.jpg and git-v.png from 21:12, has no matching cover in index.html. It still sits in renders/. tools/contact.js picks it up, and so does tools/build_gallery.py, which globs renders/*.jpg and appends non-catalog slugs. So the gallery will show a 19th cover: a second 'Git & GitHub Masterclass' whose branch lines cross into a tangled triangle.
   - *Fix:* Delete covers/01-grid/renders/git-v.jpg and git-v.png (no PDF exists), then re-run contact.js to confirm 18 covers. While in there, screenshot the .wrap element to renders/_wrap-build-llms-from-scratch.png (or .jpg). build_gallery only shows the print wrap if a _wrap* file exists, so right now the wrap is invisible outside index.html.
2. **rag-in-production** — The origin disc (r=16 centred at 36,802) sits on top of the 4px colophon rule (814–818) and pokes 10px into the left margin (its edge is at x=20). Meanwhile the quarter-arc's baseline ends at y=802, 12px above the rule. So the figure neither stands on the rule nor clears it, and the rule's clean left end becomes a blob directly above the 'Vizuara' imprint.
   - *Fix:* In FIG.cosine set ox = X(0)+16 = 46 (the disc's left edge lands on the 30px margin) and oy = Y(13)+F-16 = 786, so the disc's bottom and the arc's end share the y=802 baseline that every other figure stands on. Reduce R0 by about 16px so the arc still ends inside column 10.
3. **mathematical-foundations-for-ml** — The steep eigenvector is cut by the clipPath at y=254, leaving a flat butt end floating in empty paper around x≈250 under the title. The note says the eigenvectors 'run off the page', but one of them visibly stops at an invisible line. It reads as a rendering crop at full size and at 180px.
   - *Fix:* Stop clipping the eigenvectors to the figure field. Draw them as the two λ-scaled vectors instead: red bars from the centre along ±v1 to the ellipse's major vertices (length 1.95u) and along ±v2 to its minor vertices (0.52u), with the unit circle kept as the before-state. That shows Av = λv, which is the actual lesson, and removes the clip. If you keep the infinite lines, end both at the same deliberate distance from the centre with square caps aligned to a grid row, never at the clip rect.
4. **charlie-vision-room** — The top row of patches (y 310–354) sits flush against the ceiling wall (298–310). The bottom row of generated-pixel squares (758–802) sits flush on the floor wall (802–814). The filled top row fuses into a comb hanging from the ceiling. The hollow bottom row loses its bottom edge in the floor and reads as open cups, so the four rows of the output grid are no longer the same shape.
   - *Fix:* Keep at least one gutter (12px) between every figure element and the room walls, as the Language room does. Either shrink both grids to 3×3 patches (9 tokens in the corridor) centred in each half of the room, or keep 4×4 and inset them by 6px top and bottom (patches at 316–528, output at 584–796).
5. **5d-parallelism** — The mesh labels TP on both axes (TP/CP/DP across, TP/EP/PP down), so a book titled '5D' shows six labelled axes of parallelism. The advanced readers this book is for will count them and read it as an error, even though the intent was a 4-way TP laid out 2×2.
   - *Fix:* Make it 32 ranks = 2^5, with one binary split per axis and each label used exactly once. Use 8 columns split by TP 4px / CP 12px / DP 44px and 4 rows split by EP 12px / PP 44px, and enlarge the cells to fill the field. Alternatively keep 64 ranks, set one 'TP×4' label at the corner of the 2×2 block, and remove the second TP.
6. **diffusion-lm-from-scratch** — Denoising steps 5 and 6 (rows at y≈646 and 702) unmask nothing: no red in either row. That contradicts the caption ('tokens unmasked at each step are red') and how masked-diffusion samplers work, since they reveal a scheduled ~N/T tokens every step. At 180px the figure reads as 'fill, stall, stall, finish'.
   - *Fix:* Replace the seeded t[] with an explicit schedule that reveals 1,2,2,2,2,2,1 tokens over steps 1–7, e.g. t = [4,5,2,1,5,7,6,3,4,6,2,3]. Every row then has at least one red token scattered across the width, which also makes the 'everywhere at once' point more strongly.

## Improvements (highest impact first)

1. **system** — Seven of 18 figures are grids of 44px module squares (build-llms, ai-context, 5d, pi-vs-hermes, diffusion-lm, kernel-engineering, charlie-vision). Three of them are near-twins at 180px: pi-vs-hermes-vs-codex, diffusion-lm-from-scratch and kernel-engineering are all black ground, rows of 3px hollow squares, paper fills and one red. At 50 books the advanced shelf will read as spreadsheets. Add a programme rule: at most one in five figures may be a module matrix, and a tabular mechanism must find its non-tabular view. Concretely: keep kernel-engineering as the canonical matrix. Redraw diffusion-lm with no hollow squares, so masked positions are empty ground and revealed tokens stack up from the rule into a jagged red-capped skyline. Redraw pi-vs-hermes as described in the next item.
2. **pi-vs-hermes-vs-codex** — This is the weakest emblem: a labelled four-row table that looks like a slide and twins diffusion-lm. Rethink it as three vertical context columns standing on the colophon rule, in columns 2–3, 6–7 and 10–11, each 10 modules tall (the full window), with freed slots hollow at the top. Put the red summary block where each harness places it: Pi at the bottom under the kept recent turns, Hermes in the middle between head and tail, Codex as the only block left. Set the Pi / Hermes / Codex labels once below the columns in 13px, and drop the 'Full context' row because the column height already says it. You get three distinct silhouettes that are still true to the book.
3. **vla-world-models** — Replace the drawn robot arm with the VLA mechanism in the system's own vocabulary. On the left, a 4×4 block of observation patches (squares) and a row of instruction tokens (circles) feed a row of 7 action bars (x, y, z, roll, pitch, yaw, gripper) whose heights are the discretised action values, with the gripper bar in red. Between them, 2–3 outlined 'imagined' frames stand for the world model's predicted next observations before the robot acts. Everything sits on the grid, it reads at 180px, and it covers both halves of the title. If the arm is kept, at minimum route the dotted path clear of the imagined wrist ring and gripper, and draw the outline gripper as single 4px strokes instead of stroked 8px bars.
4. **charlie-sound-room** — The symmetric amplitude waveform is the most generic image on the shelf (every podcast app icon) and says nothing about voice agents. Keep the room and corridor, but show turn-taking: user speech bars above the corridor axis, agent speech bars below, a short silent gap where endpointing hands the turn over, and one red bar where the user barges in and the agent's bars stop dead. This is the defining engineering problem of the book's subject, drawn with the same bars.
5. **system** — The Charlie sub-series drifts from Rule 6 and from consistent imprint placement. All four covers drop the 4px colophon rule, so the 'Vizuara Books' cell at x=30 floats under nothing and the room floor (130–646) acts as a partial rule. Shorten the room by one gutter (floor at 790–802) and restore the full-width 4px rule at 814. The fourth colophon cell 'Intelligence Factory / Charlie, Book II' puts 'Charlie' where a reader expects an author credit: set it as 'Book II' bold over 'Intelligence Factory' regular. Also drop the book number from the subtitles, where it duplicates the colophon, and remove the stutter in 'Book II — Vision: Vision Transformers'. The subtitles become 'Vision Transformers', 'Inference Engineering', 'Voice Agents' and 'Reinforcement Learning, from bandits to reasoning models'.
6. **build-llms-from-scratch** — Several title breaks split noun phrases. Change build-llms-from-scratch from 'Build Large Language / Models (LLMs) / from Scratch' to 'Build Large / Language Models / (LLMs) from Scratch' (same 60px size; the longest line is about as wide as the current one, and it also moves 'Language' back from its 24px squeeze against the ribbon). Set 5d-parallelism as two lines at 60px, '5D Parallelism for / Large Model Training', instead of orphaning 'Training'. Set mathematical-foundations-for-ml as 'Mathematical / Foundations / for Machine Learning', so 'for' travels with its object. In the same pass, clean up figure tangencies. In machine-learning-fundamentals, move the boundary (adjust b) so it runs through the gutters and never half-covers a dot; 8 dots are currently ambiguous and the 3 deliberate errors get lost. In decision-trees-from-scratch, move leaf d1 from column 5 to column 6, because at 12px from c2 it reads as c2's sibling. In rag-in-production, enforce a ≥2° minimum spacing between the seeded rays, since the near-coincident pairs read as double-struck rendering errors.
