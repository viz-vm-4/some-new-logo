# 02 · Specimen Plates

**The idea.** Every Vizuara book is a numbered plate in one atlas of machine learning. Each cover
carries one specimen: the book's actual mechanism, drawn in engraved line (swelling contour
lines, hatching, cross-hatching, stipple) as if a nineteenth-century scientific atlas had
documented it. The parts are lettered *a, b, c* and keyed in a two-line italic legend at the
foot, the way Gray's *Anatomy* or Haeckel's plates are. The drawings are computed rather than
illustrated: descent paths are integrated on the actual surface, the 5D mesh is a true
penteract projection, and the attention arches are softmax weights. Around the specimen sits a
strict frame: toned paper by level, one serif, a plate number, a scale bar, the title large at
top left and the imprint small at the foot. That frame makes 50 very different drawings read as
one shelf. No animals, no people, no raster images; everything is SVG generated by the page's
own script.

**The chiaroscuro rule.** Only two inks touch a figure. Black lines swell into the shadows and
paper-white lines (knock-outs) swell into the lights; the mid-tones are the toned paper itself.
Every plate therefore prints as one spot tint plus black, reads in greyscale, and keeps its form
at 180px, where the white highlights carry the volume.

## Palette
| | hex | role |
|---|---|---|
| Ink | `#15171C` | key plate: all type and line |
| Paper white | `#FBF8F0` | knocked-out highlights (the paper showing through) |
| Sage | `#9FB7A4` | toned paper for **beginner** |
| Ingres blue | `#9EB1C7` | toned paper for **intermediate** |
| Sanguine | `#C68D79` | toned paper for **advanced** |

The three papers are mid-value tints of the same lightness, like Ingres drawing papers, so the
black and white behave identically on all of them. None of them builds on the retired
orange/sky/magenta pinwheel.

## Type
**Bodoni Moda** (Google Fonts) is the only family. Titles use weight 600 at a *text* optical
size (`opsz` 14) even at 58–64px, so the Didone behaves like a sturdy Victorian scientific
Modern (think Scotch Roman in a Royal Society journal) with print-safe hairlines, not a fashion
face. Legends, subtitles and figure letters are in italic. Plate apparatus (plate number, level,
imprint, series line) is in 11px capitals letterspaced 0.24em at `opsz` 8.

## Level
Level is carried twice. The paper colour (sage, blue, sanguine) reads across a shelf or a
thumbnail grid. A micrograph-style scale bar, top right beside the level word, has one, two or
three filled segments.

## System anatomy
Plate number (catalogue order in roman numerals) top left · level + scale bar top right · title
top left, hand-broken, with italic subtitle and an optional series line above · the specimen,
lettered · rule + legend · imprint (V-in-a-lens mark: two converging rays, *viz*, to see) and
capsules/hours at the foot. Coming-soon titles say *In preparation* instead of capsules and
hours. The **Charlie and the Intelligence Factory** sub-series adds a series line above the title
and makes each room a nineteenth-century machine.

## Emblems, one line each
- **I · AI Context Engineering**: the context window as a sediment core. The strata are system
  prompt (bedrock), tools (brickwork), retrieved docs (laminae), memory (pebbles), conversation
  (paired varves) and query (lit top), against a ruler of token positions up to the limit.
- **II · Mathematical Foundations for ML**: a two-basin loss surface as an engraved block
  diagram. Two steepest-descent paths are integrated numerically from −∇L; one reaches the
  minimum, one is caught in a local minimum.
- **III · Neural Networks from Scratch**: one unit dissected like an anatomical specimen (octant
  cutaway). Inputs arrive on filaments, weights are beads sized by magnitude, the core forms the
  weighted sum and the shell is the activation. The back cover adds a Fig. 2 network where ink
  rods are positive weights and white rods negative.
- **IV · Build LLMs from Scratch**: a decoder-only transformer raised as a column. The token
  course and embedding plinth support N identical block-drums threaded on the dowel of the
  residual stream, with the output head as the capital bearing the next-token distribution.
- **V · 5D Parallelism**: the smallest 5-D device mesh (2⁵ = 32 devices) is a penteract,
  projected in decagonal symmetry. One device's five edges (white) are the axes data, tensor,
  pipeline, sequence and expert.
- **VI · Pi vs Hermes vs Codex**: a three-armed logarithmic spiral. Each agent's history winds
  inward, older spans sealed into ever smaller chambers (compaction), with a white thread through
  every chamber (memory). It is a structural comparison, not a claim about any agent's ratios.
- **XIV · Build Decision Trees from Scratch**: a binary tree pressed like a herbarium sheet, with
  split conditions at the nodes and leaves pale or dark by class. Fig. 2 is the same four splits
  partitioning the plane.
- **XXXIV · Transformers**: one causal attention head as an arc diagram (arch width = softmax
  weight, the last query in white). Fig. 2 is the same weights as a Hinton diagram.
- **L · Harness Engineering** (coming soon): an armillary sphere with the model at the centre and
  rings for the agent loop, tools and memory, held by the horizon (sandbox) and the frame
  (permissions).
- **XLVI · Charlie I, Language Room**: the paged KV cache as a card catalogue. Drawers are pages,
  each card is one token's K/V, some pages are free, and a ledger on top is the page table.
- **XLVII · Charlie II, Vision Room**: a lens casts an image, the screen is cut 4 × 4, and the
  patches are read off as a numbered sequence (ViT patch embedding as Victorian optics).
- **XLVIII · Charlie III, Sound Room**: Scott's phonautograph (horn, membrane, stylus, sooted
  drum). Fig. 2 unrolls the trace and cuts it into frames.
- **XLIX · Charlie IV, Reasoning Room**: a five-armed bandit whose winning reward distribution
  sprouts a tree of reasoning steps, with the rewarded path in white.

## Print notes
Minimum ink line is 0.6px at trim (≈0.45pt) and minimum knocked-out line is 0.9px. Figure letters
carry a paper-coloured halo so they survive dense hatching. The cover prints as two inks: the
level's spot tint as a solid, and black, with white knocked out. The PDFs in `renders/pdf/` are
vector at the exact 7.5 × 9.25in trim. The page also contains a full print wrap for Plate III
(back with blurb, Fig. 2 and the start of the plate catalogue; a 58px spine; front).
