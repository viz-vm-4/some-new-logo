# Art-director review — 02-plates

**Score:** 8/10

**Verdict:** This is a disciplined, ownable system: computed engravings sit inside a strict atlas frame that reads as one family at 180px, and the figures are unusually accurate (the penteract is a real 32-vertex, 80-edge 5-cube, the Fig. 2 partition matches all four tree splits, and the ViT patch strip matches the inverted screen image cell for cell). It is held back by two font fallbacks, legend lines that strand key letters, and three emblems (Pi, Reasoning Room, Harness) that say less, or something less true, than the rest.

**Strongest:** charlie-language-room is the strongest cover. The card catalogue is an exact picture of the paged KV cache: drawers are pages, cards are one token's K/V each, blank-label drawers are free pages and the ledger on top is the page table. It is a single object with a strong silhouette that still reads at 180px, and it is squarely in the nineteenth-century machine vocabulary. decision-trees-from-scratch comes a close second for rigour: the thresholds 0.50, 0.35, 0.65 and 0.78 and the pale/dark classes in Fig. 1 map exactly onto the partition in Fig. 2.

**Weakest:** pi-vs-hermes-vs-codex is the weakest cover. It is the only emblem that is an abstract pattern rather than a thing or a mechanism. The three interleaved arms cannot be traced past the first turn, so the "vs" in the title never shows. The centre collapses into a solid black vortex that moirés on the 180px sheet. The arms also have visibly different live spans and chamber spacing (Codex's white live span is about 1.7× Pi's), which reads as a factual claim that the NOTES say the figure does not make.

## Must fix

1. **decision-trees-from-scratch** — Font fallback. The split labels ('x₁ ≤ 0.50', 'x₂ ≤ 0.35', and so on, index.html l.709-713) and the Fig. 2 axis labels 'x₁'/'x₂' (l.785-786) use Unicode subscript digits U+2081/2082, which Bodoni Moda lacks. The PDF embeds LiberationSerif-BoldItalic, and at 2× the subscripts are visibly heavier and differently drawn than the Bodoni italic around them.
   - *Fix:* Set the subscripts with Bodoni's own digits: x<tspan baseline-shift="sub" font-size="68%">1</tspan> (or a dy offset). Re-render and confirm that `strings renders/pdf/decision-trees-from-scratch.pdf | grep FontName` lists only BodoniModa.
2. **mathematical-foundations-for-ml** — Font fallback in the legend. The ∇ in 'one step along −∇L' (l.1436) is not in Bodoni Moda, so the PDF embeds DejaVuSans: a monoline sans nabla in the middle of Didone italic. The U+2212 minus also sets as a short raised dash, so the phrase reads '- ∇L'.
   - *Fix:* The simplest fix, and a better one for a beginner book, is to rephrase as 'c one step down the gradient'. If the symbol must stay, draw the nabla as a small inline SVG path matched to the italic's stem weight. Re-render and check that the PDF font list is Bodoni-only.
3. **ai-context-engineering** — The legend's first line ends on a bare key letter: '…two laminae a turn; c' / 'recalled memory; …'. build-llms-from-scratch has the same fault ('…one of N; d' / 'the residual stream…'). The letter is cut off from its gloss on two core-six covers. Milder splits of the same kind appear on math ('b the / minimum'), harness ('e the / horizon'), vision ('c the / image') and decision-trees ('Fig. 2. The / plane').
   - *Fix:* In faceHTML, bind each key to its first word: b.cap.replace(/<\/b> /g, '</b>&nbsp;'). Also bind 'Fig.&nbsp;2.&nbsp;The', and add `text-wrap: pretty` to .face .legend. Re-check that every legend still fits in two lines.
4. **pi-vs-hermes-vs-codex** — The three arms use different live-context spans and chamber spacing: dths = [0.44, 0.58, 0.74] at l.939. In the render, Codex's white live span is visibly about 1.7× Pi's, with correspondingly fewer, larger chambers. A reader will take this as a claim that Pi compacts most often and Codex keeps the most live context. NOTES says the figure makes no such claim, and nothing sources those numbers.
   - *Fix:* Either take each agent's compaction threshold and cadence from the book's own findings and name them in the legend, or set all three dths to one value so the figure really is structure-only.
5. **charlie-reasoning-room** — The encoding misleads for an RL book. Each arm's reward distribution is plotted on its own unmarked 76px slot, so the means (0.35/0.52/0.70/0.30/0.46) show only as small sideways shifts with no axis. What the eye sees is that the pulled arm has the tallest, narrowest hill (sd 0.10). That says 'lowest variance', not 'highest expected reward', and the arm to pull is chosen on expected reward.
   - *Fix:* Put all five distributions on one shared reward scale. Either rule a common reward axis along the cabinet top with a mean tick under each hill, or stand the densities up as vertical violins against one ruled scale. Either way, the pulled arm's mean should be visibly the highest; grow the tree from that mean tick.
6. **charlie-vision-room** — The screen measures about 170×250px, so the 4×4 grid (and the 16 tiles in Fig. 2) are portrait rectangles. ViT patches are square P×P ('an image is worth 16×16 words'), and this is the one visual fact a ViT reader will check.
   - *Fix:* Make the screen square (for example 220×220, moving the lens and bench to suit) so both the grid cells and the Fig. 2 tiles are square. The cell-by-cell match between patches and screen, which is currently correct, will then carry over.

## Improvements (highest impact first)

1. **system** — Make the legend a robust slot. It is pinned at top:801px with the imprint about 14px below its second line, so any three-line legend in the next 40 books will hit 'VIZUARA BOOKS'. Anchor it from the bottom (bottom:58px, growing upward, with the rule placed above it rather than at a fixed 792px). Add a build-time check that fails if a legend exceeds two lines. Make the &nbsp; key-binding from must-fix #3 the default in faceHTML rather than a per-book edit.
2. **system** — Make plate numbers permanent. Today the numeral is ORDER.indexOf(slug)+1 over the scraped catalogue order, so inserting or reordering one book renumbers every later plate, and printed copies, spines and the back-cover 'From the Atlas' index stop agreeing with the web. Store a fixed `plate` number per book (append-only, new titles take the next free numeral). Add [100,'C'] and [90,'XC'] to toRoman before the catalogue passes 89.
3. **pi-vs-hermes-vs-codex** — Rethink the emblem as a comparative plate in the Haeckel manner: three sectioned nautilus shells side by side at the same scale, lettered Fig. 1a Pi, 1b Hermes, 1c Codex. Chambering stays the metaphor for compaction, and the white memory thread becomes the siphuncle, the real tube that runs through every chamber of a nautilus, which is anatomically exact. Three separate objects make the 'vs' readable at 180px, and the black moiré vortex at the centre goes away.
4. **harness-engineering** — The armillary is six rings of near-equal weight. At 180px it reads as a generic globe or atom, the agent loop (b) is indistinguishable from memory (d) and the horizon (e), and the b and c leader dots land about 26px apart on the same ring cluster. Consider Watt's centrifugal governor, the nineteenth-century device that turned a raw engine into a self-regulating machine by feedback: a engine shaft (the model), b spindle and flyballs (the observe-act loop), c linkage to the throttle valve (tool calls), d stop collar (permissions), e housing (sandbox). It is one clear silhouette and true to 'the layer that turns a model into an agent'. If the armillary stays, cut it to three rings with a clear weight hierarchy: a heavy agent-loop ring with visible arrowheads and hairline-free secondary rings.
5. **system** — Ration the engraved ball-and-rod node. It already appears in 6 of 13 covers (NN inputs, 5D devices, Transformer tokens, tree nodes, the Reasoning tree, Harness tool beads), and across 50 books the atlas will drift into a shelf of molecule models. Write a rule into the style guide: spheres only where the node is a physical thing (a unit, a device). Tokens, tree nodes and graph vertices get flat engraved roundels, ticks or type, as a real plate would letter them.
6. **system** — Tighten two apparatus rules. (1) The level bar's rects abut exactly (x=0.75+i*17, width 15.5, stroke 1.5, so the gap is zero), which makes Advanced one solid bar and Intermediate a 2/3 bar with no countable segments. Because the three papers are equal-lightness by design, this glyph and the word are the only level cue in greyscale, so set x=0.75+i*19 for a clear 2px gap. (2) Title sizes currently run 64/60/58/56 across 13 covers. Limit them to two steps (64 and 56) and add a break rule against ending a line on 'for': 'Mathematical / Foundations / for Machine Learning' fits at 64 (about 635 of 647px).
