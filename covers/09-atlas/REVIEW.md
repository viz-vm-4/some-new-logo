# Art-director review — 09-atlas

**Score:** 8/10

**Verdict:** Interchange is a rigorous transit-map system that nobody else could own: every tick matches a real capsule (I spot-checked this), the titles are loud and legible at 180px, and the fonts and trim are clean. It is held back by three emblems that are technically wrong (the maths resultant, a transformer block with only attention, a git fork that runs backwards), a Charlie sub-series that is one map in four colours, and a level code that only separates out the advanced books.

**Strongest:** 5d-parallelism: the five parallelism axes radiate from one hub over a grid of GPU blocks. ZeRO shares data parallelism's corridor, and the capstone "Putting It All Together" is a circle line that literally composes the axes. It is exact to the subject, the tick counts are right (40), it reads instantly as a shape at 180px, and the night ground signals advanced before you read a word. It is the clearest proof of the system's promise that the subject's architecture is the geography.

**Weakest:** neural-networks-from-scratch: the seven lines just swap order between three layer bars. That draws a braid or sorting network, not the "fully connected net" the caption claims: each line visits exactly one slot per layer, so there is no many-to-many wiring at all. At 180px it is also a near-twin of ai-context-engineering (a parallel rainbow bundle entering from the left edge with bullets 0–n stacked in a column). For a core-six title it neither shows the mechanism nor stands apart on the shelf.

## Must fix

1. **mathematical-foundations-for-ml** — The 'resultant vector' (lime, part 7, Putting It Together) runs exactly along the z-axis. It leaves the origin at 45° up-right, on the same line as the teal z-axis (both are 45° diagonals through the origin), so it reads as the negative z half-axis, not as a sum of x, y and z. The emblem misstates the one piece of maths it claims to show.
   - *Fix:* Make the resultant the single deliberate break from the octilinear grid (a free-angle arrow, e.g. about 28° up-right, ending near (430,430)). Drop dashed 2px ink guide lines from its tip to the ground plane and to the x and z axes, so the parallelepiped of components is visible. State the exception in the legend: 'the only line off the grid, because it is the sum.'
2. **build-llms-from-scratch** — The tinted box labelled 'transformer block ×12' holds only the attention branch (q, k, v). A GPT-2 block is attention plus a feed-forward (MLP) sub-layer, each with its own residual add. The MLP is missing, and it is where the book's 'Layer Norm, GELU, and Shortcut Connections' capsule lives. Repeated ×12, the diagram describes the wrong architecture.
   - *Fix:* Add a second, shorter branch on the orange residual stream above the attention lozenge (for example from y≈420 to y≈470), leaving and rejoining the stream the same way. Label it 'mlp' in the territory italic and move one of part 3's ticks onto it. Nudge the attention lozenge down about 30px to make room. The box then reads as attention + MLP, both residual.
3. **git-github-masterclass** — 'your fork' (lime, part 7) branches off main at x≈630, then turns and runs back left to x≈140. On a commit graph that means the fork's commits come before the point it forked from, so time runs backwards. It also ends in a dead-end terminus, although the tagline promises 'to a merged pull request'.
   - *Fix:* Branch the fork off main earlier (x≈470), run it left to right on a lower track at y≈560 with its ticks, then bring it back up into main at x≈660 through a ring labelled 'pull request' (or run it off the right edge into an 'upstream' band). Put bullet 7 at the fork's start and move 'TO OPEN SOURCE' to its right-hand end.
4. **system (all covers)** — Key and map bullets use white numerals on every ink. On saffron (3) and lime (7), and on the lifted night versions #F0B232 and #A8C63D, contrast is about 2:1. Desaturated (checked on the 5d-parallelism and ai-context-engineering keys), the 3 and 7 all but disappear. In greyscale or single-colour print the numeral is the only link between a line and its key row, so the table of contents stops working.
   - *Fix:* Set the numerals in ink #16181B on the saffron and lime bullets, day and night (as the NYC subway does on its yellow N/Q/R/W bullets). Keep white on the darker inks. Apply the same rule to the map bullets and the Charlie series bullet if a light ink is ever used there.
5. **decision-trees-from-scratch** — The italic 'regression' label sits about 8px directly above the orange 'TO FORESTS & BOOSTING' label at x≈520–670, y≈517–537. The two read as one two-line label, and 'regression' appears to name the orange wrap-up line rather than the green subtree. Separately, the title break 'Build Decision / Trees from Scratch' splits the compound noun 'Decision Trees'.
   - *Fix:* Mirror 'classification': place 'regression' outside the green subtree at the same offset from its branch (about x=560, y=610, beside the right-hand green branch), and keep 'TO FORESTS & BOOSTING' alone above the orange line. Re-break the title as 'Build Decision Trees / from Scratch'. It fits at the current size (about 577px out of the 647px available).
6. **ai-context-engineering (print wrap)** — The back-cover stats read '43 capsules · 200 figures · about 10 hours'. '200 figures' is hardcoded in index.html (buildWrap, about line 793) and is not in the catalogue data. It is an invented fact on a print deliverable.
   - *Fix:* Remove '200 figures' or replace it with a value from the data (e.g. '8 parts'). Drive every figure on the back cover from DATA, as the front-cover stats line already does.

## Improvements (highest impact first)

1. **neural-networks-from-scratch** — Rethink the emblem around the training loop the book actually builds. The forward pass runs left to right through the input, hidden and output layer bars (parts 0–3) to a 'loss' terminus, then turns down and returns right to left underneath as the backward pass (gradients), closing a loop back at the input bar. The code half (parts 5–6) runs a second, inner lap of the same loop, which is 'Training the Entire Network'. It is true to the mechanism, it uses only existing vocabulary (layer bars, rings, a loop line), and it gives the cover a closed-loop silhouette that no longer echoes ai-context-engineering's bundle.
2. **charlie-language-room, charlie-vision-room, charlie-sound-room, charlie-reasoning-room** — The four volumes share one factory plan and only change which line is lit, so no emblem comes from the book's subject, and at 180px they look like one cover in four colours, which is the brief's core complaint about the old covers. Keep the shared plan, but shrink it to a locator inset (a 'you are here' panel) and let each lit line end in a room drawn from its subject: Language, the line threading a grid of paged KV-cache blocks; Vision, a 4×4 patch grid the line scans row by row ('Patches are tokens'); Sound, the line stepping as a waveform through VAD, ASR and TTS stations; Reasoning, the agent–environment loop as a circle line. Also lock the frame to the 40px grid (it currently runs x=25 to 682: 25px left, 38px right) at a fixed y on all four; it now sits 10px lower on III and IV (top 305 vs 295) because the foot is shorter.
3. **system** — Level is only really encoded for advanced books. Beginner and intermediate are both day maps told apart by a 10.5px ● or ■ in the header, which is about 2.6px at 180px, so the shelf reads two levels, not the three the notes claim can be read 'from across the room'. Within the system: enlarge the trail-grade glyph to about 24px and hang it at the header's right margin (about 6px at thumbnail size). Or give intermediate a second marker, such as a doubled 3px header rule. Keep day and night for advanced.
4. **system** — Make the bullet and tick grammar consistent, as the legend promises. dsa-in-python shows only bullet 0 (lines 1–8 are unlabelled, and the pink terminus has no 8), git-github-masterclass omits 1–3, maths and LLMs float bullets mid-field (the LLMs '1' sits alone inside the transformer block), and pi-vs-hermes-vs-codex numbers 1–3 in red, blue and green where every other cover has 1 = blue. Rule: every line gets its bullet at its first station, and numbers always follow ink. Enforce a minimum tick pitch of about 10px in the router and make the audit fail below it. Git's orange main crams four ticks into about 18px twice, and the second room of charlie-reasoning-room has three ticks at an 8px pitch, against 14–17px in its other rooms.
5. **system** — Plan against repetition before scaling to 50. Parts are coloured by order, so every map opens red, blue, green, saffron, and 9 of the 10 non-series covers enter with a red line from the left edge. Distinctness rests entirely on silhouette, and ai-context-engineering, neural-networks-from-scratch and pi-vs-hermes-vs-codex already share the 'rainbow bundle from the left' shape. When building the parametric templates, cap the bundle template at about 1 in 8 of the catalogue, log each map's silhouette type in the audit, and vary the entry point (top trunk as in decision-trees, central hub as in 5d-parallelism, spiral, loop), so a 50-cover grid does not become a wall of left-edge rainbows.
6. **system** — Print hardening for the night maps and the bleeds. Night tints (#21262D and #2A3038 on #15181D, about 1.2:1) carry meaning on 5d-parallelism (the GPU grid), pi-vs-hermes-vs-codex (the context-window and memory territories) and the Charlie room segments. They will fill in on press on a rich-black ground and vanish in greyscale, so raise them to about #2B313A and #363D47. Lines and grounds are designed to bleed, but the PDFs are exact trim (540×666pt), so export with 0.125in (9px) of bleed, extending the ground and every edge-running line past the trim.
