# Art-director review — 10-wildcard

**Score:** 8/10

**Verdict:** "Some Assembly Required" is a real, ownable system: one frame, tab, badge and card/plastic logic on every cover. Every title reads at 180px, and several emblems (5D H-tree, Decision Trees, Math alphabet sprue, Sound Room) are excellent. The revision needs to fix a font fallback on four covers, four technical misstatements (LLM part count and mask size, the wrap's mask arrow, the DeiT teacher routing, Charlie I's K6/V6), a handful of part and label collisions, and yellow-on-primer greyscale.

**Strongest:** 5d-parallelism. Here the metaphor and the subject are the same object. A balanced (H-tree) runner is how real moulds feed many cavities equally, and here its five binary branchings are the five parallelism axes (DP/PP/CP/EP/TP) feeding 2^5 = 32 numbered GPU dies. It is exact rather than decorative. At 180px it reads as a crisp white circuit on carbon that no other cover could be confused with, and it shows the whole system working: Styrene White plastic = Systems, Carbon card and 3 pips = advanced.

**Weakest:** pi-vs-hermes-vs-codex. The three rows (history bar, then trapezoid, then summary bar) are deliberately identical, so at 180px they read as three yellow batteries or spreadsheet rows. The compaction is only about 2:1 (316px history to about 165px summary), so the book's actual idea, squeezing a long history down, is barely visible. The "Memory" half of the subtitle ("Context Compaction and Memory") is not represented at all.

## Must fix

1. **neural-networks-from-scratch / decision-trees-from-scratch / kernel-engineering / charlie-language-room** — Font fallback. The Unicode subscripts (x₁…x₃, h₁…h₄, ŷ₁ ŷ₂, C₁₂, K₆, V₆) and '≤' in 'x ≤ t' are not in the loaded Archivo. The PDFs embed LiberationSans-BoldItalic (NN, Decision Trees, Language Room) and LiberationSans-Bold (Kernel). At 2x the subscripts and ≤ are visibly thinner and narrower than the Archivo ExtraBold Expanded around them. Liberation is a local system font, not a Google font, so it will differ on another machine.
   - *Fix:* Stop using the U+2080 subscript range and U+2264. Set each subscript as a normal Archivo digit in an SVG <tspan> at about 62% size with dy≈+0.28em. For ≤, either draw it as a 2-stroke path at the label's stroke weight or set that one glyph in IBM Plex Mono, which is already loaded and has U+2264. Re-render and confirm `strings *.pdf | grep FontName` lists only Archivo and IBMPlexMono.
2. **build-llms-from-scratch** — Factual mismatch in the emblem. NOTES and the caption say '20 capsules, 20 parts', but the tree has 19: 1 mask + 12 blocks + 6 tokens (to/be/or/not/to/?). The causal mask is 8×8 (N=8 in the code) while the sequence it masks has 6 positions. The wrap's front mask repeats this.
   - *Fix:* Set N=6 so the staircase has one column per token part, and ideally align the mask columns over the six token parts. Add a truthful 20th part, for example a token-embedding plate on the runner between the tokens and the block stack, or an LM-head/softmax plate beside '?'. Otherwise drop the '20 parts' claim everywhere.
3. **build-llms-from-scratch (wrap, back cover)** — Assembly step 2 'Mask the future' shows the lower-triangular staircase, then an arrow, then a full square grid. That runs backwards: masking takes the full score matrix and removes the upper triangle.
   - *Fix:* Swap the two: full grid, then arrow, then staircase. Alternatively, show the full grid with the upper-triangle cells as empty sockets. Use the same 6×6 size as the front.
4. **deit-from-scratch** — The CNN teacher's runner drops from the last teacher part and joins the token runner at the DIST token, which says the teacher's output is the distillation token's input. In DeiT the DIST token is a learned embedding like CLS. The teacher's prediction is the target that supervises the distillation head's output.
   - *Fix:* Route the teacher runner down the right side (x≈645) into the DISTILLATION HEAD plate (y≈716) instead of into the token row, and optionally mould 'TARGET' on that gate. Feed DIST from the token runner exactly as CLS is fed.
5. **cnn-fundamentals** — The smallest feature-map stack at the far right overlaps the frame's right rail: its two back layers sit on top of the rod (confirmed at 2x). Every stack is also drawn with exactly 3 layers (k=2..0), so the 'pyramid' shrinks spatially while channel depth stays constant. In a real CNN, channels grow as the maps shrink.
   - *Fix:* Tighten the stack step (x += s+30 to about s+20) or scale the sizes so the last stack clears the rail by at least 16px, like every other part on the shelf. Make depth grow as size falls, for example 2,3,4,5,6,8 layers, so the pyramid tells the true story.
6. **ai-context-engineering** — The moulded labels 'SYSTEM' and 'MEMORY' run edge to edge of their parts with no side padding; SYSTEM touches both bevels. Every other label has air around it.
   - *Fix:* Widen those two parts, and their matching tray slots, to text width plus 2×10px. Alternatively, drop letter-spacing on moulded labels to about .06em when the part is under 70px wide.
7. **ai-context-engineering (and every future intermediate Agents book)** — Greyscale failure. Chrome Yellow #F2B41E on Grey Primer #A4A39B converts to about 181 vs 162 grey (Δ≈19/255). In a greyscale proof the whole sprue flattens into the card and is held only by the offset shadow. Six of the nine Agents titles are intermediate, so this will recur.
   - *Fix:* For yellow on Primer, lighten the moulding toward #FFD43B (about 206 grey, Δ≈44). Also darken the edge pass to about #6E4A00 at 2px so the silhouette holds. Encode this as a card×plastic rule in the Sprue API (see improvements).
8. **5d-parallelism** — The level plates collide with the dies. 'PP' sits on the pins of dies 17 and 18, 'TP' sits on the pins of 28 and 29, and 'EP' butts into 07's pins. The text is legible, but the plates look jammed in. Also, ranks are numbered row-major, so the labelled sub-trees cover non-contiguous ranks (the EP group is {00,01,08,09}). Real TP/EP/CP groups are contiguous rank blocks.
   - *Fix:* Move each plate to the midpoint of a runner segment clear of the pins. For example, put TP on a vertical stub, or widen the leaf-pair gap by about 12px so the TP plate fits between the pins. Number the dies in H-tree leaf order, so TP pairs are 2k,2k+1, EP quads are 4k..4k+3, and so on.
9. **charlie-language-room** — The next token '?' is shown hollow (not yet made), but K₆ and V₆ are drawn as solid finished parts. The keys and values for position 6 cannot exist before token 6 is produced, so the state is contradictory.
   - *Fix:* Draw K₆ and V₆ in the same hollow, unmoulded treatment as '?', so the whole 6th column reads as pending.
10. **neural-networks-from-scratch** — Both hidden layers are labelled h₁–h₄ identically, which is ambiguous notation for a 3-4-4-2 network.
   - *Fix:* Label the layers h⁽¹⁾₁…h⁽¹⁾₄ and h⁽²⁾₁…h⁽²⁾₄, using tspans as in the fallback fix. If that is too dense at 22px, use h₁…h₄ and g₁…g₄.
11. **kernel-engineering** — The rotated TEST SHOT stamp's lower edge just grazes the sprue frame's top rail (about 0–3px). It reads as an accident rather than a stamp placed on top.
   - *Fix:* Choose one. Drop the stamp about 18px so it clearly overprints the frame's top-right corner, as a real rubber stamp would, or lift it to leave at least 12px of clear card. Put the rule in the spec so all three coming-soon covers match.
12. **system (renders/_sheet.png)** — The committed full-page screenshot is corrupt. A dark block clips the page header to 'Some Assemb', and the 52-spine shelf shows only 7 spines plus a colour strip. The live page renders correctly (I checked it separately); this is a Chromium tile artifact on a 25,298px-tall 2x capture, but anyone reviewing the PNG will see a broken page.
   - *Fix:* Regenerate the sheet at deviceScaleFactor 1, or capture the page in sections (header, core set, wrap, shelf) instead of one fullPage 2x shot.

## Improvements (highest impact first)

1. **pi-vs-hermes-vs-codex** — Rethink the emblem (weakest on the shelf). Keep 9 parts and no ranking claim, but make the compaction and the memory visible. One long history runner carries about 24 small message tiles and branches into three compactor nozzles moulded PI / HERMES / CODEX. Each nozzle ends in one short summary part (about 1/10 of the history's length) plus one tab-shaped MEMORY file part. That gives 3×3 = 9 parts, a roughly 10:1 squeeze readable at 180px, and both halves of the subtitle.
2. **modern-robot-learning** — Rethink the emblem. Links, servo horns and a gear say 'robot', not 'learning', and it is the cover closest to the generic robot imagery the brief retires. Make the demonstrated trajectory the main runner, as R's fitted line is. Gate numbered action-chunk parts (short arrow segments a₁…a₈) along it, with a camera-observation tile feeding a π(a|o) policy plate at the start and the gripper as the final part at the goal. Keep at most one arm link as hardware.
3. **ai-context-engineering** — Sharpen the concept from typed slots to a token budget. At 180px the typed-slot tray plus nine labelled boxes reads like an org chart. Make the tray a single 0–128K ruler socket, and make each part a bar whose length is proportional to its tokens (SYSTEM 2K, TOOLS 8K, MEMORY, DOCS, HISTORY, USER). Their combined length should visibly overrun the tray by about 1.5×, so choosing what goes in is legible at a glance, and the labels get room to breathe.
4. **system** — Guard against labelled-brick monotony. About half of the 16 emblems (context, LLM tokens, Pi, DeiT, Kernel, Language Room, CNN, Vision Room) are rectangles on straight runners, and the next 17 Agents and Language titles will drift the same way. Add a spec rule: every emblem must have a unique silhouette in a blurred 180px thumbnail and at most one row of labelled rectangles. Favour non-rectangular part shapes (glyphs, discs, curves, trees) as Math, NN, Decision Trees, R, Sound and Reasoning do.
5. **system** — Write a card×plastic contrast floor into the Sprue API: at least 35/255 greyscale difference between plastic body and card, with a mandated dark keyline or alternate tone when a pairing fails. It already bites on Yellow/Primer, and it will break outright on Styrene White (#EEECE5) on Bone (#EEE8DC), where they are almost identical, and on Yellow/Bone the day a beginner Systems or Agents title is added. Test every one of the 7 plastics × 4 cards once and publish the matrix on the page.
6. **charlie-language-room / charlie-vision-room / charlie-sound-room / charlie-reasoning-room** — Tidy the sub-series signals. 'Book I' is shown three times (kit line '· I', tab '· I', subtitle 'Book I ·'), while the series name 'Charlie and the Intelligence Factory' never appears in full. Set the kit line to 'CHARLIE & THE INTELLIGENCE FACTORY · I' (it fits left of the badge at 12.5px mono) and drop 'Book I ·' from the subtitle. That leaves just the room and topic, for example 'Language — Inference Engineering', and also shortens the Reasoning subtitle so its title sits lower and less crowded under the top row.
