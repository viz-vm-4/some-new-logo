# Final proof — 10-wildcard

All 12 must-fix items are verified in the renders and the PDFs, which embed only Archivo and IBM Plex Mono, and the dimensions, capsule/hour counts, levels and kit numbers all match books.json. One blocker remains: the overlapping action-chunk arrows on Modern Robot Learning. There are also six minor label, routing, glyph-tone and edge-hairline issues.

Review must-fixes confirmed fixed: 12

## Defects

- **modern-robot-learning** (blocker) — Action-chunk arrows a1-a5 overlap one another. Their centres are only 23, 31, 41 and 47 px apart, but each arrow is 50 px long, because they are offset 64 px onto the inside of the trajectory's first bend. The heads of a1-a3 are hidden under the next arrow's shaft, so the start of the demo reads as a pile of fused parts instead of 8 separate chunks. That contradicts the NOTES claim that they are 'spaced by arc length'. Confirmed at 2x.
  - *Fix:* In EMBLEMS['modern-robot-learning'], space the 8 chunks by arc length along the offset (parallel) curve, not along the trajectory. Alternatively, move them to the convex (lower-right) side of the path. Either way, adjacent arrows must be at least 60 px apart centre to centre. Check that no two arrow polygons intersect, then re-render the JPG, PNG and PDF.
- **deit-from-scratch** (minor) — The moulded 'TARGET' label overflows its 50 px plate: the first T sits on the left bevel and the last T runs into the right edge where the teacher runner joins, leaving about 1-3 px of padding. This is the same fault as review item 6, which NOTES says is now a rule of at least 10 px side padding.
  - *Fix:* Move the TARGET plate onto the vertical teacher runner (x=624 in emblem coordinates, around y=300) and widen it to text width plus 2x10 px. If it has to stay in the 58 px gap, set it at 8.5 px with wd 100 and ls .06 so it clears both bevels.
- **deit-from-scratch** (minor) — The CLS runner (horizontal at emblem y=376) crosses DIST's vertical runner (x=109). Runners render fused, so this draws a 4-way junction that joins CLS and DIST to both heads. That contradicts the caption and NOTES ('CLS goes to the class head and DIST to the distillation head'), which is the routing review item 4 was about.
  - *Fix:* Reroute DIST so it does not cross, for example S.runner([[109,338],[109,348],[493,348],[493,358]],5), which runs in the 20 px gap under the token row into the top of DISTILL HEAD. Keep CLS's runner as it is.
- **modern-robot-learning** (minor) — On the a1-a8 plates (17 px tall, 11 px text), the lowered subscript digits sit on or cross the plate's bottom bevel, with no bottom padding. This is visible at 2x on a1, a2, a4, a7 and a8.
  - *Fix:* Raise the plate height to about 21 px, or lift the label baseline about 2 px. Alternatively, reduce the subscript dy from +0.28em to about +0.18em for these plates.
- **decision-trees-from-scratch** (minor) — The drawn replacement glyphs use the groove style, not the moulded-text style, so they read lighter and thinner than the text beside them. On Decision Trees, '≤' is filled #9E2F1F with about 1.9 px strokes, while 'x' and 't' are #6C2015 ExtraBold. On Robot Learning, 'π' is #6040A0 at 2.2 px while '(a|o)' is #422C6E, and π sits about 18 px away from '(a|o)'. The fallback is gone, but the mismatch that review item 1 described persists in a new form.
  - *Fix:* Draw the ≤ and π paths with the text colour (T.txt) at about 2.6-3 px stroke, with the same +0.8 px T.hi offset copy that S.text uses, instead of S.groove. Close the π to '(a|o)' gap to about 3 px so it reads as π(a|o).
- **kernel-engineering** (minor) — The TEST SHOT stamp overprints the frame corner as intended, but the frame's top rail, with its white highlight line, runs straight through the small second line 'COMING SOON' and cuts the tops of its letters. At 1x that line is hard to read.
  - *Fix:* Keep the overprint but move the stamp about 8-10 px, so the rail crosses the stamp's border or the gap between its two lines, not the 'COMING SOON' glyphs. Alternatively, put a Carbon-coloured knockout behind the 'COMING SOON' text only.
- **raster-renders** (minor) — Several JPG and PNG renders have a full-width 1-device-pixel dark hairline at the trim edge that is not in the PDFs, most likely page background showing through at a sub-pixel screenshot offset. It is on the top edge of build-llms (JPG row 0 grey 115 vs card 162), cnn (109 vs 162) and neural-networks (160 vs 232). It is on the bottom edge of decision-trees (142 vs 232), r-masterclass (142 vs 232), ai-context-engineering (146 vs 162), mathematical-foundations (208 vs 232) and _wrap.jpg (140 vs 163). The dark cards show a faint version.
  - *Fix:* Snap each .cover and .wrap to whole-pixel page coordinates before the screenshot (integer offsets and margins, or clip the screenshot to a rounded box), then re-render with tools/render.js. Confirm that the first and last pixel rows of every JPG equal the card colour.
