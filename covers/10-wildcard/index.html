Vizuara calls itself a library for building things. Its books are taught in capsules, and half the titles end in “from Scratch”. So every Vizuara book is a kit.

The emblem on each cover is an injection-moulded parts tree, and it is drawn from the book's own subject: a causal mask, a network whose runners are its weights, a balanced runner that feeds 32 GPUs through five levels of parallelism. It's ready to snap off and assemble. The box is the system: the same frame, maker's tab and skill-level badge on every cover. The plastic tells you the series and the card tells you the level.

  • 01 · Card = levelBone for beginner, Grey Primer for intermediate, Carbon for advanced. The badge repeats the level in pips, so it still reads in greyscale.
  • 02 · Plastic = seriesEach subject family is moulded in one colour, so a shelf sorts itself by colour.
  • 03 · The sprue = the subjectThe book's real mechanism is laid out as parts. Where it fits, one part stands for each capsule.
  • 04 · Kit numberEvery book gets a catalogue number (VZ-001 to VZ-052), and it's moulded on the maker's tab. Collect the set.
  • 05 · Test shot = coming soonUnreleased books are shown as a clear-plastic pre-production test shot.
  • 06 · Factory EditionThe Charlie sub-series is moulded in candy colours on cocoa card, numbered I to IV.

Titles are set in Archivo ExtraBold at 112.5% width, with tight leading and hand-broken lines, then fitted to the measure. The kit data, badge and contents line are in IBM Plex Mono SemiBold. Text moulded into the plastic uses Archivo ExtraBold Expanded. The imprint is a new Vizuara mark: a V moulded as a tiny sprue, with two parts gated into one injection point. It can also be read as two inputs feeding one neuron.

Card × plastic contrast floor

This rule is built into the Sprue API. The moulded plastic and its card must differ by at least 35/255 in greyscale. When a pairing falls short, the plastic is re-moulded lighter or darker along its own hue and gets a heavier keyline, so every cover survives a greyscale proof. Pairings marked ● are adjusted.

The whole library · 52 kits on one shelf

Every book in the catalogue has a kit number, a series plastic (the band at the top of the spine) and a level card. The three coming-soon titles are clear test shots, and the Charlie books sit together on cocoa. Shelved by series and then by level, the library sorts itself into bands of colour and still reads as one family.
