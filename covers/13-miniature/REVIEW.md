# Art-director review — 13-miniature

**Score:** 8/10

**Verdict:** Muraqqa is a distinctive, carefully worked system: the folio structure gives Vizuara a shelf nobody would mistake for anyone else's, and most emblems are technically exact. It is held back by one font fallback on a core-six cover, a few encodings that fail in greyscale, and grounds that reuse the level pigments, which muddles the level code at thumbnail size.

**Strongest:** mathematical-foundations-for-ml. The miniaturist's tipped-up floor is the linear map itself. A=[[1,½],[0,⅘]] is applied correctly: Ae2=(½,⅘), the square becomes a right-leaning parallelogram and the round pool becomes an ellipse, with non-scaling strokes. The tradition and the maths are one idea, and it reads at 180px. It only needs the ⅘ glyph fix below. Honourable mention: kernel-engineering, where the forthcoming book is an unfinished miniature and the tiled matmul is exactly right (row-major, 4 blocks done, block (1,1) under the brush, aligned with A's row-panel and B's column-panel).

**Weakest:** pi-vs-hermes-vs-codex. Three near-identical columns of generic bars that could sit on any comparison book. At 180px they read as three dark-blue slabs. The only difference between them is where a 5px gold "press" sits. The memory jewels are 10px dots floating below, not connected to anything. The subtitle's two nouns (compaction and memory) are present in the code but not visible as a mechanism.

## Must fix

1. **mathematical-foundations-for-ml** — Font fallback. U+2158 (⅘) is not in Eczar, so it renders in DejaVu Sans. The PDF embeds BAAAAA+DejaVuSans only for that glyph. It shows as a thin sans fraction next to the Eczar ½, 1 and 0. The 'A=' is also set with no space, so the gold '=' touches the A.
   - *Fix:* Don't use Unicode vulgar fractions. Set both fractions as stacked Eczar numerals (4 over 5 and 1 over 2, ~17px, with a 2px ivory bar) so all four entries are in one face, or write 0.5 / 0.8. Set 'A =' as one text run with a real space. Re-render and confirm the PDF font list shows only Eczar and Anek Latin.
2. **neural-networks-from-scratch** — The weight-sign encoding fails in greyscale. Vermilion threads (relative luminance ≈0.16) sit on a malachite ground (≈0.11), and in a greyscale conversion every negative weight disappears, leaving only the gold threads. The same red-on-green pairing fails for deuteranopes. The back-cover colophon also says of each stone 'its colour its activation', but the stone colours are random (r()>.45 ? saffron : pink).
   - *Fix:* Draw each negative thread over an ink underlay 1.6px wider, the way the SQL threads are drawn, or make negatives dashed (e.g. 5 3), so sign survives in grey. Then either run a real forward pass through the drawn weights and colour the stones by activation, or rewrite the colophon to say what the colours actually mean.
3. **5d-parallelism** — The TENSOR (4px lapis) and CONTEXT (3px malachite) key swatches sit on the ink ground at about 1.5:1 contrast. They are faint in colour and vanish in greyscale, so two of the five axes cannot be decoded from the key. The key spacing is also uneven: fixed 1/5-width slots leave a ~70px gap after DATA and ~15px after the other labels.
   - *Fix:* Put the key on an ivory band (the mesh's own ivory, with the same gold/ink edge) so each swatch looks exactly as it does in the mesh. Lay out the five items by measured width with equal ~24px gaps, centred under the mesh.
4. **build-llms-from-scratch** — The attention ramp [lapisD, lapis, sky, ivory, gold] is not monotonic in lightness. The top step (gold, the attention-sink column) is darker than step 4 (ivory), so the diagonal reads as the strongest attention and the sink as weaker. In greyscale, gold and sky merge. Even in colour, lapisD and lapis are hard to tell apart, so the 5-step scale reads as about 3 steps.
   - *Fix:* Use a ramp that gets lighter at every step, e.g. ink → lapis → sky → goldL → ivory, with the maximum as the lightest. If gold should mark the sink, use it as a bezel or outline on each row's argmax cell, not as a fill step.

## Improvements (highest impact first)

1. **system** — Keep the three level pigments for the hashiya only. At present 4 of 13 grounds reuse them: ai-context-engineering (intermediate) sits on a peori ground, neural-networks and mathematical-foundations (both beginner) sit on malachite and lapis, and deit (advanced) sits on malachite. At 180px the ground covers about 70% of the cover and the margin is a 50px band (checked on the contact sheet). So ACE reads as a yellow beginner book and Math as a blue advanced one. Take grounds only from hingul, lakh, kajal, gulabi and asmani, plus 2–3 new non-level pigments (e.g. geru red-ochre ~#9C4A2B, surmai slate ~#4E5660, olive ~#6B6A2E). Write a rule for assigning them (ground by subject family) so 50 books don't get arbitrary pairings. Also make the stipple texture consistent: it currently appears only on math and nn, so apply it to every ground or to none.
2. **system** — Title measure and line breaks. On sql-masterclass, kernel-engineering, pi-vs-hermes-vs-codex and 5d-parallelism the title hits the fitter's limit (ink spans x≈118–603), about 14px from the cartouche's inner vermilion rule. ACE and the Charlie titles have ~90px. Cap the measure at ~430px (C.w−160) so every title keeps ≥45px from the rule. Re-break the long titles:
- 'Build Large / Language Models / (LLMs) from Scratch', so 'Large Language' no longer ends a line on its own.
- 'Mathematical / Foundations / for Machine Learning', so 'for' no longer dangles.
- '5D Parallelism' as the hero line, with 'for Large Model Training' as the .sub line at 0.58em (as on Pi), so the main line can be set larger.
3. **pi-vs-hermes-vs-codex** — Rethink the emblem around its mechanism. Keep the three stelae, but make compaction visible as geometry: the old-turn bars get thinner and denser as they approach a much larger press (not a 5px bar) and come out as the gold summary block. Set each memory jewel in a niche beside its stele, with a gold thread back to the specific old turn it was taken from. Make the three compaction ratios differ boldly (e.g. summary blocks at ~10%, 20% and 35% of height) so the comparison reads at 180px without claiming which agent does what.
4. **charlie-language-room** — The 'paged KV cache' is not actually shown. The niches fill contiguously in row-major order, and the page dividers are 1px goldL lines that disappear on lapis (one is orphaned against the right column), so the wall reads as a post-office wall of pigeonholes. Show the block table instead:
- Group the niches into visible pages of 4 (3px gutters inside a page, 14px between pages).
- Allocate the pages out of order and leave some free pages empty.
- Run gold threads from each run of floor tokens up to its non-adjacent page.

The floor then holds the logical sequence and the wall the physical memory, which are the room's two planes and the whole point of paging.
5. **charlie-vision-room** — The Vision Room shows the same thing as deit-from-scratch: the garden cut into patches, then a CLS + patch strip. At 180px both covers read as 'garden picture plus a strip of tiles'. Also, the 17 floor tokens (~24px) sit straight on a checker floor of the same scale and dissolve into it. Give the room a ViT-specific mechanism that DeiT doesn't have: tint the 16 wall patches by the class token's attention (gold on the cypresses, lapis on the sky) and run gold threads from the CLS tile to the top 3 patches. Lay the floor sequence on a solid ink or lapis runner so it separates from the checker.
6. **5d-parallelism** — Technical accuracy of the nesting order. Tensor-parallel partners, which need the fastest links, are drawn two cells apart, while Expert is the innermost split. Reorder AX to Pipeline (h,4, heaviest rule) > Data (v,2) > Expert (v,2) > Context (h,2) > Tensor (v,2, finest rule). That is still an 8×8 mesh of 64 devices, it matches Megatron's tp-cp-ep-dp-pp rank order, and 'thinner rule = closer, faster link' becomes true for an advanced reader.
