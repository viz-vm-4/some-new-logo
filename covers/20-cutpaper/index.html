Every Vizuara book is a small construction of cut, folded and layered coloured card, and the construction is the book's idea: a window you can only see part of the context through, neurons pinned together with brass fasteners, a context history pleated into a concertina.

The system is strict so the pictures can be free. Board colour tells you the shelf, the ivory title slip is always in the same place, and the number of sheets stacked under the slip (one, two or three ply) tells you how deep the book goes. Light always comes from the top left. Everything is vector SVG with procedural grain and computed shadows. On press it becomes real die-cuts and laminated board, so the shadows stop being printed and become real.
