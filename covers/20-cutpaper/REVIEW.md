# Art-director review — 20-cutpaper

**Score:** 7.5/10

**Verdict:** Paperworks is a distinctive and well-built system: the Matisse palette has conviction, the fixed ivory slip holds the shelf together, every title reads at 180px, the fonts load correctly and all data matches books.json. It is let down by a level signal too thin to read in the grid, an overcrowded and partly wrong 5D emblem, and a few colour collisions that break its own ply and greyscale promises.

**Strongest:** neural-networks-from-scratch. The construction is the idea: each weight is a strip whose width is |w|, ivory for positive and ink for negative, and the neurons are held with brass paper fasteners. It is accurate (a full 3-4-2 network, 20 strips), reads instantly at 180px, holds up in greyscale (yellow, red and ivory nodes stay separate values), and it is the one cover on the shelf you would want to pick up. It shows what the whole paper vocabulary can do.

**Weakest:** 5d-parallelism. The five planes overlap by roughly 40%. Four of them are near-identical ivory/mist greys, and a red expert tile shows through gaps in the sequence plane as a stray red 'I' shape. At full size it reads as a mosaic of rectangles, not five planes cut five ways. At 180px and in greyscale it becomes one pale staircase. The data-parallel plane is also drawn wrong (see must-fix). This is a core-six book and currently the weakest cover in the set.

## Must fix

1. **rag-in-production** — The only level signal visible in a thumbnail is lost. The ultramarine drawer front ends at y=532, and the single ultramarine ply sheet starts at y=535. The two blues touch across a 3px sliver of mustard and read as one blue mass, so at 180px this intermediate book looks like it has no ply band.
   - *Fix:* Give RAG's second sheet a colour that contrasts with the drawer (ink), or lift the drawer about 20px (D.y 370 -> 350) so board shows between drawer and band. Better still, adopt the shelf-level ply colour rule in improvement 1.
2. **5d-parallelism** — The data-parallel plane is technically wrong. By the system's own vocabulary, 'Cut = split or partition', and every other plane's cut means the model is partitioned across devices (tensor = columns, pipeline = stages). Cutting the data plane into a 2x2 grid therefore says the model is sharded four ways, which is 2D tensor sharding. Data parallelism replicates the whole model and splits the batch. The caption's 'replica blocks' does not match the drawing.
   - *Fix:* Draw the data plane as two or three identical, uncut sheets stacked with a small offset (replicas), or as one uncut sheet with a stack of thin batch slips fanned beside it. Keep the 'cut' operation for the four dimensions that really do partition (tensor, pipeline, sequence, experts).
3. **pi-vs-hermes-vs-codex** — The emblem reverses its own caption. NOTES says 'recent turns stay flat and legible, older history is pleated', but each strip has its legible lines at the top and its concertina at the bottom. Every chat transcript reads top to bottom, oldest first, so a technical reader sees the recent end compacted and the old end kept.
   - *Fix:* Flip the construction. Hang the three strips from the top edge, bleeding off the top (the system allows this): pleated history at the top, flat printed turns below, and the loose ends stopping at different heights above the slip. The comparison between the three methods then shows in the ragged bottom edge, like three receipts.
4. **mathematical-foundations-for-ml** — The emblem fails greyscale, although NOTES says greyscale was checked. Vermilion #D93D2B and Leaf #22704B have almost identical luma (about 93/255 each), so the red and green distributions merge into one mid-grey mass. The lemon curve (luma about 211) almost disappears into the ivory graph paper (about 237). In a greyscale proof the 'three distributions' read as a two-hump blob.
   - *Fix:* Separate the three curves by value, not just hue. For example, change the right-hand curve from leaf to plum #55284A (luma about 52) or ink, keep vermilion for the tall curve, and either darken the left curve (cobalt) or give the lemon piece a thin ink cut edge. Check again with a desaturated proof.
5. **inference-engineering** — The pressed-out vermilion '04' page (spot 468,462, r=14) slides under the vermilion top ply sheet (y=522) and fuses with it into one red shape. This blurs the advanced/two-sheet count, and the '04' label sits jammed against the band edge.
   - *Fix:* Move that piece clear of the slip (e.g. to about 470,400) or recolour it (cobalt or mint), so no emblem piece of the same colour touches a ply sheet. Add that as a system rule.

## Improvements (highest impact first)

1. **system** — Make level readable in the grid and make it rule-based. Each extra sheet peeks only 13px (about 3px at 180px), so one band and two bands are nearly indistinguishable in the contact sheet, and the 'INTERMEDIATE/ADVANCED' label is about 3px tall there. Band colours are also chosen per book (ultra, ink, lemon+verm, verm+ink, leaf, saff, rose+ink…), so they carry no meaning, and some nearly vanish: saff on kraft on charlie-sound-room, ultra beside night on ai-context-engineering. Fix: raise the peek to 20–22px per sheet, and fix the two ply colours per shelf in a table, never per book (e.g. sheet 2 = ink, sheet 3 = lemon; ivory for sheet 2 on the Night shelf, vermilion for sheet 3 on the Mustard shelf). Add one rule: no emblem piece of a ply colour may touch the ply sheets.
2. **system** — Lock title size and let the subtitle give way first. fitTitles currently shrinks the title to make room for two-line subtitles. Measured sizes: charlie-reasoning-room 59px against 74px for its three siblings, and inference-engineering 59px against 76px for ai-context-engineering. That is visible in the thumbnail row. Change the fit order: shrink the subtitle to about 20px and cap it at one line before touching the title. Use 'Reason: Reinforcement Learning' on the Reasoning front (move 'from bandits to reasoning models' to the back), and set the Inference subtitle to one line ('Batching, KV-cache, paged attention, speculative decoding'). Also re-break 5d-parallelism as '5D Parallelism for / Large Model Training'. I measured 'Large Model Training' at 580px at the current 64px, inside the 588px measure, so the break that splits 'Large / Model' and the weak 'for Large' middle line both go, at no size cost.
3. **5d-parallelism** — Rebuild for legibility. Reduce overlap so each plane shows at least 60% of itself (e.g. planes about 180x210 with the same diagonal step), and give each plane its own stock colour, alternating light and mid values so neighbours separate in greyscale: tensor lemon, pipeline cobalt, data ivory (as stacked replicas), sequence rose, experts mist with vermilion active tiles. Keep expert tiles from showing through gaps in the plane in front. The test: someone can count five planes in the 180px thumbnail.
4. **charlie-reasoning-room** — Rethink the emblem. A staircase up to a flag is the stock 'progress/success' pictogram and says nothing about RL or 'from bandits to reasoning models'. Alternative in the same paper language: keep the step-fold, but make it a pop-up branching tree of steps (several reasoning traces). Only one branch reaches the vermilion reward token, and the other branches are folded flat to the floor (pruned by reward). A row of three pull-tab levers on the left wall, each with a small stack of punched counters of different heights, stands for the bandit starting point.
5. **charlie-sound-room** — Rethink the emblem. The floor cards alternate ink/ivory bar by bar, so they read as a bar chart or graphic-EQ, and the ivory and ink waves on the back wall tangle into a squiggle at thumbnail. Make it about voice agents, i.e. turn-taking: keep one row of standing cards but group them into two turns, an ink utterance (the user), a clear empty gap (end-of-turn detection, the agent listening), then an ivory reply with a different envelope. Replace the double wave on the wall with a single clean cut-edge wave, or remove it.
6. **system** — Tighten the stage floor and ration the tile motif before scaling to 50. NOTES says constructions always tuck under the slip, but reinforcement-learning's grid ends at y=462, leaving about 73px of dead board above the band while neighbours end within 10–30px (NN 526, 5D 512) or tuck under (ACE, RAG). Set one rule: the lowest piece tucks under the slip or stops a fixed 24px above the top sheet, and move the RL grid down about 50px or enlarge the tiles to about 104px. Also, rows or grids of small rectangles already carry 8 of 13 emblems (ACE, Build LLMs, 5D, Pi, Language Room, Inference, RAG, RL), and at 180px ACE, 5D, Inference and RL all read as 'grid of tiles'. With about 12 vermilion Language-shelf books still to come, limit the tile grid to about 1 emblem in 5 and lean on pleat, loop, pin, curve and weave, which already make the most memorable thumbnails (NN, Math, Build LLMs).
